Disclaimer

This personal website shares my mathematics notes and a curated collection of learning, study and research resources. The following applies throughout the website.

Notes and editorial guidance

My notes are personal study materials, not official course publications, and may contain errors or omissions. Resource descriptions, reading paths and skip suggestions are editorial guidance rather than a compulsory curriculum, assessment or endorsement of every opinion expressed by a linked author.

Sources and verification

Entries show their sources, review dates and available evidence. “Verified” identifies the stated sourced fact; “Partially verified” means some details remain unresolved; “Historical” identifies an older year or intake; “Not published” and “Not verified” identify missing or unconfirmed information. This is a curated directory, not an exhaustive or continuously monitored database. Follow the latest corresponding institutional notice when a policy or call changes.

Study, funding and fee status

Application dates and status badges reflect recorded notices, not live admissions portals. Date-only deadlines have no confirmed closing hour. Admission, scholarships, tuition categories, immigration status and employment eligibility have separate requirements. A student visa or residence alone does not automatically establish local fees. Background matching is a navigation aid, not an admission prediction, transcript assessment or legal determination. Institutions decide eligibility and awards; neither a listing nor an enquiry guarantees admission or funding.

Researchers and student records

An affiliation or public email does not establish supervision eligibility, an available student place or a complete appointment history. Group membership and individually published research interests are labelled separately. Public student records describe the documented event, not necessarily recruitment; no public record does not mean a researcher is not accepting students. Coverage counts describe this directory, not national faculty totals or rankings. Contact researchers individually and thoughtfully, without automated or bulk enquiries.

External resources and copyright

Access badges describe how a resource can be read: a direct PDF, online text, institutional access, preview or source page. Free access does not grant redistribution or translation rights. Authors and publishers retain their rights, and the linked source’s terms apply. External links normally open a new tab; their content, availability and policies are controlled by their operators.

Privacy and corrections

The optional background and learning-path tools do not upload or automatically save your answers or study progress. Theme preference is saved in your browser. Linked websites have their own privacy practices. If you find an error or an outdated entry, please email me with the relevant page and source.
