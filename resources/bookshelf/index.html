My bookshelf

Keep a local reading list with a reading status and your own page or chapter position.

Saved reading

Bookshelf settings and backups

Your list stays in this browser without an account or sync. Clearing browser data removes it; export a backup to keep a copy. Exports contain resource IDs, reading statuses and manual positions. Application answers are not stored.

Import accepts version 1 bookshelf JSON, up to 8 MiB and 2,000 entries, and merges by resource type and ID. Existing progress wins on duplicates; missing catalogue IDs are retained.
