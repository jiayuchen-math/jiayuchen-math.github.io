US mathematics PhD applications

Advice for applying to PhD programs in mathematics

Zajj Daugherty · English · Faculty application advice

A substantial guide to advanced coursework, research opportunities, letters, personal statements and funding. It also discusses master's and bridge routes for students who need more preparation before applying to a doctorate.

Start planning with the preparation and letters sections, then make a separate checklist for each target program.

Testing schedules, bridge programs and funding eligibility linked in an undated guide require current program-specific checks.

Graduate School FAQ: preparing a mathematics application

UC San Diego Department of Mathematics · English · Department guidance

The personal-statement section asks applicants to describe mathematical preparation, contributions and independent study with substance. It gives concrete advice on relevance, explaining setbacks briefly and obtaining honest feedback on a draft.

Read the personal-statement questions as prompts for evidence, not as a template to copy.

The general testing discussion is not a statement of every program's current requirements.

Advice for those applying for PhD Programs in Math

AcademicOverAnalysis and r/math commenters · English · Forum advice & discussion · 2023

A long discussion of research experience, recommendation letters, earlier weak grades and returning to study after employment. Read replies as well as the opening post to see where experiences and opinions differ.

Useful for identifying questions to ask faculty who know your mathematical work.

The poster's professional identity was not independently verified; individual anecdotes are not admission thresholds or guarantees of funding.

A 2022 pure mathematics and mathematical physics application diary

2022申请季：纯数学与数学物理（导读标题）

Skadiologist (Zhihu display name) · Chinese · Zhihu applicant experience · 2022 cycle; updated 2022-08-16 in the reviewed rendering

A first-person diary covering preparation, recommendation letters, interviews, rejections and alternatives across US and European programs. The account follows interests in mathematical physics toward a reported decision to study algebraic geometry at Warwick.

Read the timeline and changes of plan rather than treating the reported grades or individual results as an admissions bar.

Historical self-reported outcomes, not independently verified admissions records; the display title is editorial.

France & European masters

A personal account of applying for mathematics study in France

马马其关于法国留学的经验与信息分享

Qi Ma / 马骐 · Chinese · Applicant / student experience

A signed account by a student who reports studying a funded M1 in Toulouse in 2023–24. It describes master's applications, separate scholarship applications and the later search for doctoral supervision and funding.

Use the experience to build questions about your own M1/M2 route, scholarship process and doctoral plans.

Some broad claims about institutions, application platforms and admission chances are the writer's interpretations, not verified current rules; the document's own date is not stated.

Checked 2026-10-16

ALGANT alumni testimonials

Daniele Turchetti; Yongqi Liang; Ilaria Lovato; Ariyan Javanpeykar; Djordjo Milovic · English · Signed alumni accounts

Several alumni describe studying across countries, choosing courses, writing a master's thesis and finding their place in mathematical research. The accounts illustrate different experiences within a joint mathematics program rather than a single admissions recipe.

For understanding the academic and personal experience of a mobile European master's program.

Selected alumni testimonials are not a representative outcomes survey, current course catalogue or funding offer.

Advice on preparing an application

Conseils pour candidater

University of Strasbourg, UFR de mathématique et d'informatique · French · Department application guidance

A department's concrete advice for presenting a CV and motivation letter, with good and bad examples. One example shows how to explain a change of university rather than leaving the reader to guess why it happened.

English reading guide: focus on explaining your academic path and reasons clearly; the examples are not a ready-made motivation letter.

Mathematics master's entrance exams at Tokyo and Tokyo Tech

東大/東工大 数学科 院試体験記

すい / sui · Japanese · Applicant exam account

An algebra/number-theory-oriented writer compares written and oral exams at two institutions and describes a staged past-paper study group. The account includes differences between the basic and specialist papers rather than only listing a final result.

English reading guide: look at how practice changed over the months and how the writer revised weak areas alongside the chosen specialty.

The historical Tokyo Tech name is retained from the original; exam weighting and pass-threshold impressions are not official rules.

Not passing the Tokyo mathematics entrance exam: a fourth-year account

A writer identifying as a Hiroshima mathematics undergraduate recounts preparation and exam-day experiences at Tokyo and Osaka Metropolitan, with interests in mathematical statistics. Including an unsuccessful Tokyo attempt offers a different perspective from success-only accounts.
