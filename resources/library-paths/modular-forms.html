Calculus

Use limits, derivatives, integrals and series; distinguish computation from justification.

Calculus, Volume 1 — Gilbert Strang and Edwin 'Jed' Herman. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can solve representative differentiation, integration and convergence exercises and explain the hypotheses used.

Proofs & Mathematical Writing

Read quantified statements; write direct, contrapositive, contradiction and induction arguments.

Book of Proof — Richard Hammack. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can explain the quantifiers in a statement and write these forms of proof without relying on a worked solution.

Real Analysis

Prove convergence, continuity, compactness and differentiation/integration results.

Basic Analysis I — Jiří Lebl. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can work with epsilon arguments and prove standard compactness and convergence results, not just calculate limits.

Complex Analysis & Several Complex Variables

Study holomorphic functions, contour integration, series and residues.

Guide to Cultivating Complex Analysis: Working the Complex Field — Jiří Lebl. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can apply Cauchy’s theorem and residue methods with hypotheses; several-variable material may require further preparation.

Linear Algebra

Work with vector spaces, bases, linear maps, kernels, rank and eigenvalues.

Linear Algebra — Jim Hefferon. Open online text / archive

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can prove rank–nullity, change bases and distinguish diagonalizable maps from general linear maps.

Abstract Algebra & Galois Theory

Use groups, rings, ideals, quotient constructions and field extensions.

Abstract Algebra: Theory and Applications — Thomas W. Judson. Open online text / archive

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can construct quotient groups and rings correctly and state the assumptions of the isomorphism theorems.

Modular Forms & Arithmetic Geometry

Study modular transformations, q-expansions and basic examples before arithmetic applications.

Modular Functions and Modular Forms — J. S. Milne. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can work with holomorphic functions and group actions; check the arithmetic and representation prerequisites of advanced texts.

Research reading: additional tools

Choose according to the particular text, not as an obligation to complete every foundation again.

Elementary Number Theory

Use divisibility, congruences, Euclid’s algorithm and elementary reciprocity examples.

Elementary Number Theory: Primes, Congruences, and Secrets — William Stein. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can prove elementary arithmetic statements and solve congruences, not just apply memorized formulas.

Commutative Algebra

Study modules, localization, prime ideals and Noetherian arguments.

A Primer of Commutative Algebra — J. S. Milne. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can localize a ring or module, work with prime ideals and check exactness in basic module examples.

Algebraic Number Theory & Class Field Theory

Study number fields, rings of integers, ideals and local arithmetic.

Algebraic Number Theory — J. S. Milne. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can use Dedekind-domain and field-extension arguments; class field theory and cohomology may require more background.
