Proofs & Mathematical Writing

Read quantified statements; write direct, contrapositive, contradiction and induction arguments.

Book of Proof — Richard Hammack. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can explain the quantifiers in a statement and write these forms of proof without relying on a worked solution.

Linear Algebra

Work with vector spaces, bases, linear maps, kernels, rank and eigenvalues.

Linear Algebra — Jim Hefferon. Open online text / archive

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can prove rank–nullity, change bases and distinguish diagonalizable maps from general linear maps.

Abstract Algebra & Galois Theory

Use groups, rings, ideals, quotient constructions and field extensions.

Abstract Algebra: Theory and Applications — Thomas W. Judson. Open online text / archive

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can construct quotient groups and rings correctly and state the assumptions of the isomorphism theorems.

Representation Theory & Lie Theory

Study group actions on vector spaces and basic representation decompositions before Lie or tensor-categorical references.

Introduction to Representation Theory — Pavel Etingof et al.. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can construct representations, intertwining maps and basic decompositions; infinite-dimensional and Lie-theoretic preparation must be checked separately.

Research reading: additional tools

Choose according to the particular text, not as an obligation to complete every foundation again.

Category Theory

Understand functors, natural transformations, limits and adjunctions through examples.

Category Theory in Context — Emily Riehl. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can express a universal property and verify a natural transformation or an adjunction in a concrete example.
