Proofs & Mathematical Writing

Read quantified statements; write direct, contrapositive, contradiction and induction arguments.

Book of Proof — Richard Hammack. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can explain the quantifiers in a statement and write these forms of proof without relying on a worked solution.

Linear Algebra

Work with vector spaces, bases, linear maps, kernels, rank and eigenvalues.

Linear Algebra — Jim Hefferon. Open online text / archive

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can prove rank–nullity, change bases and distinguish diagonalizable maps from general linear maps.

Calculus

Use limits, derivatives, integrals and series; distinguish computation from justification.

Calculus, Volume 1 — Gilbert Strang and Edwin 'Jed' Herman. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can solve representative differentiation, integration and convergence exercises and explain the hypotheses used.

Point-Set Topology

Understand open sets, continuity, products, quotients and compactness.

Topology Without Tears — Sidney A. Morris. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can prove statements using these definitions and produce basic separation or compactness counterexamples.

Differential & Riemannian Geometry

Work with smooth manifolds, tangent spaces, differential forms and metrics.

What Is Differential Geometry? Curves and Surfaces — Anton Petrunin and Sergio Zamora Barrera. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can change coordinates, use the tangent map and compute exterior derivatives; curvature and geometric-analysis gaps may remain.

Abstract Algebra & Galois Theory

Use groups, rings, ideals, quotient constructions and field extensions.

Abstract Algebra: Theory and Applications — Thomas W. Judson. Open online text / archive

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can construct quotient groups and rings correctly and state the assumptions of the isomorphism theorems.

Algebraic Topology & Homotopy Theory

Study fundamental groups, covering spaces, homology and cohomology.

Algebraic Topology — Allen Hatcher. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can compute standard fundamental groups and homology groups; stable homotopy and higher categories require additional bridges.

Symplectic Geometry

Learn symplectic forms and Hamiltonian vector fields before Floer or contact-topological references.

Lectures on Symplectic Geometry — Ana Cannas da Silva. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can use differential forms and Hamiltonian examples; pseudoholomorphic curves and Floer theory need their own analytical preparation.
