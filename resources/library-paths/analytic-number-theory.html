Proofs & Mathematical Writing

Read quantified statements; write direct, contrapositive, contradiction and induction arguments.

Book of Proof — Richard Hammack. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can explain the quantifiers in a statement and write these forms of proof without relying on a worked solution.

Elementary Number Theory

Use divisibility, congruences, Euclid’s algorithm and elementary reciprocity examples.

Elementary Number Theory: Primes, Congruences, and Secrets — William Stein. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can prove elementary arithmetic statements and solve congruences, not just apply memorized formulas.

Calculus

Use limits, derivatives, integrals and series; distinguish computation from justification.

Calculus, Volume 1 — Gilbert Strang and Edwin 'Jed' Herman. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can solve representative differentiation, integration and convergence exercises and explain the hypotheses used.

Real Analysis

Prove convergence, continuity, compactness and differentiation/integration results.

Basic Analysis I — Jiří Lebl. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can work with epsilon arguments and prove standard compactness and convergence results, not just calculate limits.

Complex Analysis & Several Complex Variables

Study holomorphic functions, contour integration, series and residues.

Guide to Cultivating Complex Analysis: Working the Complex Field — Jiří Lebl. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can apply Cauchy’s theorem and residue methods with hypotheses; several-variable material may require further preparation.

Analytic Number Theory

Combine arithmetic functions, asymptotic estimates and complex methods.

Notes on Analytic Number Theory — Kiran S. Kedlaya. Open online text / archive

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can manipulate arithmetic functions and justify basic complex-analytic estimates; sieve methods need a separate bridge.
