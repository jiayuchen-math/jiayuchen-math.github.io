Calculus

Use limits, derivatives, integrals and series; distinguish computation from justification.

Calculus, Volume 1 — Gilbert Strang and Edwin 'Jed' Herman. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can solve representative differentiation, integration and convergence exercises and explain the hypotheses used.

Proofs & Mathematical Writing

Read quantified statements; write direct, contrapositive, contradiction and induction arguments.

Book of Proof — Richard Hammack. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can explain the quantifiers in a statement and write these forms of proof without relying on a worked solution.

Real Analysis

Prove convergence, continuity, compactness and differentiation/integration results.

Basic Analysis I — Jiří Lebl. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can work with epsilon arguments and prove standard compactness and convergence results, not just calculate limits.

Measure & Integration

Build measures, measurable functions, integrals and convergence theorems.

Measure, Integration & Real Analysis — Sheldon Axler. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can apply monotone/dominated convergence and Tonelli/Fubini with their hypotheses checked.

Linear Algebra

Work with vector spaces, bases, linear maps, kernels, rank and eigenvalues.

Linear Algebra — Jim Hefferon. Open online text / archive

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can prove rank–nullity, change bases and distinguish diagonalizable maps from general linear maps.

Functional Analysis

Work with Banach/Hilbert spaces and bounded linear operators.

Introduction to Functional Analysis (18.102) — Richard Melrose. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You can use completeness, duality and basic operator examples; verify the specific text’s spectral or measure requirements.

Fourier & Harmonic Analysis

Study Fourier methods, maximal operators and singular integrals.

The Fourier Transform and its Applications — Brad Osgood. Direct open PDF

Compare resources in this subject

Can skip if… / selective review

You are comfortable with Lp estimates, Fourier transforms and interpolation; advanced multiplier topics remain separate.
