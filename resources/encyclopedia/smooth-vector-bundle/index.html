Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

A smooth vector bundle is a locally trivial smooth family of vector spaces whose changes of fiber coordinates are linear.

Chinese translation, usage & terminology

Prerequisite concepts: Smooth Manifold · Vector Space · Linear Map

Reading routes: Manifolds, curvature and symplectic geometry

Overview

A smooth vector bundle assigns a vector space to every point of a smooth manifold. Nearby fibers admit smoothly varying coordinates, but these coordinates may twist when carried around the base. Tangent vectors, covectors, and differential forms all fit this framework. It separates the linear algebra performed at a point from the global problem of making compatible choices everywhere.

Prerequisites

Use smooth manifolds, invertible linear maps, and changes of coordinates. A vector space has addition and scalar multiplication; a manifold need not. The distinction matters because the total space of a bundle is a manifold even when it has no natural vector-space structure.

Intuition

A local trivialization provides a temporary common coordinate system for nearby vector spaces. Transition matrices describe how to translate between such systems. Vectors in one fiber can be added intrinsically. Comparing vectors in different fibers requires additional information, such as a connection; local coordinates alone do not give a preferred global comparison.

Important properties

A rank-rrr bundle is trivial exactly when it has rrr smooth sections that form a basis in every fiber. Proof sketch: a frame defines (p,a1,…,ar)↦∑aisi(p)(p,a_1,\ldots,a_r)\mapsto\sum a_i s_i(p)(p,a1​,…,ar​)↦∑ai​si​(p). In local trivializations its inverse is matrix inversion, hence smooth. A trivialization gives the frame in the reverse direction.

Conversely, smooth invertible transition matrices satisfying the cocycle identities reconstruct a bundle by gluing local products. The identities ensure that identification is consistent on triple overlaps. Duals, direct sums, and tensor products are constructed by applying the corresponding linear-algebra operations to these transitions. Pullback along a smooth map f:N→Mf:N\to Mf:N→M gives fibers (f∗E)q=Ef(q)(f^*E)_q=E_{f(q)}(f∗E)q​=Ef(q)​.

Connections and further reading

Differential forms are sections of exterior powers of the cotangent bundle. Connections add differentiation of sections, while characteristic classes detect some obstructions to global triviality. Read Nicolaescu, §§2.1.4–2.1.5 for cocycles and frames, and Gualtieri, §5 for pullbacks and bundle operations.
