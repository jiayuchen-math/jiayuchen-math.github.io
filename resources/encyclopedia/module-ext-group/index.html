模的 Ext 群

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Ext groups are derived Hom groups whose first degree classifies extensions of modules with fixed end terms.

Chinese translation, usage & terminology

Prerequisite concepts: Chain Complex of Modules · projective module · Short Exact Sequence of Modules

Reading routes: Algebra toward geometry and homological methods

Overview

The functor Hom⁡R(M,−)\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,-)HomR​(M,−) preserves kernels, but may fail to carry a surjection to a surjection. Ext measures the missing exactness. Its degree-one part also answers a concrete classification question: how can a module MMM be realized as a quotient with a prescribed kernel NNN? Higher degrees retain information about longer systems of relations.

Prerequisites

Know modules, Hom, short exact sequences, and chain-complex homology. A projective module has the lifting property against surjective homomorphisms. A projective resolution of MMM is an exact augmented sequence ⋯→P1→P0→M→0\cdots\to P_1\to P_0\to M\to0⋯→P1​→P0​→M→0 with each PiP_iPi​ projective. Such resolutions exist for modules over every unital ring, by repeatedly taking free modules surjecting onto kernels.

Definition and conventions

Fix a unital ring RRR and left modules M,NM,NM,N. Choose a projective resolution P∙→MP_\bullet\to MP∙​→M and apply Hom⁡R(−,N)\operatorname{Hom}_R(-,N)HomR​(−,N). This reverses arrows, giving a cochain complex with degree-nnn term Hom⁡R(Pn,N)\operatorname{Hom}_R(P_n,N)HomR​(Pn​,N) and differential f↦f∘dn+1f\mapsto f\circ d_{n+1}f↦f∘dn+1​. Define, for n≥0n\geq0n≥0,

Ext⁡Rn(M,N)=Hn(Hom⁡R(P∙,N)).\operatorname{Ext}^n_R(M,N)=H^n\bigl(\operatorname{Hom}_R(P_\bullet,N)\bigr).ExtRn​(M,N)=Hn(HomR​(P∙​,N)).

The result is an abelian group, and an RRR-module when RRR is commutative. It is contravariant in MMM and covariant in NNN. In degree zero it is naturally Hom⁡R(M,N)\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,N)HomR​(M,N). Specifying the base ring matters: the same underlying abelian groups can have different module structures and different Ext groups.

Intuition

The resolution replaces MMM by modules on which lifting works. Applying Hom exposes which candidate maps on generators fail to respect relations, and which remaining discrepancies are removable. Taking cohomology keeps exactly the obstructions left after those corrections. Resolving the first variable is therefore a computational construction, not an extra structure attached permanently to MMM.

Examples and non-examples

For an integer m≥2m\geq2m≥2, use the free resolution

0⟶Z→mZ⟶Z/mZ⟶0.0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{m}\mathbb Z\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z\longrightarrow0.0⟶Zm​Z⟶Z/mZ⟶0.

For an abelian group NNN, the resulting Hom complex is N→mNN\xrightarrow{m}NNm​N. Consequently

Ext⁡Z1(Z/mZ,N)≅N/mN,\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathbb Z}(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z,N)\cong N/mN,ExtZ1​(Z/mZ,N)≅N/mN,

while degree zero is {n∈N:mn=0}\{n\in N:mn=0\}{n∈N:mn=0} and all degrees at least two vanish. Thus Ext⁡Z1(Z/m,Z)≅Z/m\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathbb Z}(\mathbb Z/m,\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z/mExtZ1​(Z/m,Z)≅Z/m even though Hom⁡Z(Z/m,Z)=0\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(\mathbb Z/m,\mathbb Z)=0HomZ​(Z/m,Z)=0.

For m=2m=2m=2, a nonzero extension class is represented by the displayed short exact sequence itself. It cannot split: a section would send the nonzero class in Z/2\mathbb Z/2Z/2 to a nonzero element of order two in Z\mathbb ZZ, which does not exist. The split extension has middle module Z⊕Z/2\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2Z⊕Z/2.

Ext is not symmetric in its inputs. Since Z\mathbb ZZ is projective over itself, Ext⁡Z1(Z,Z/m)=0\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathbb Z}(\mathbb Z,\mathbb Z/m)=0ExtZ1​(Z,Z/m)=0, whereas the reversed group above is nonzero. A single vanishing Ext group also does not establish projectivity: Ext⁡Z1(Z/m,Q)=0\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathbb Z}(\mathbb Z/m,\mathbb Q)=0ExtZ1​(Z/m,Q)=0 because multiplication by mmm on Q\mathbb QQ is surjective.

Important properties

Resolution independence follows from the comparison theorem: projectivity constructs chain maps lifting the identity between two resolutions, and those lifts are unique up to chain homotopy. Homotopic maps induce equal cohomology maps, so the resulting identifications of Ext are canonical.

For fixed MMM, a short exact sequence 0→N′→N→N′′→00\to N'\to N\to N''\to00→N′→N→N′′→0 gives a long exact Ext sequence. Its start contains the connecting map

Hom⁡R(M,N′′)⟶Ext⁡R1(M,N′).\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,N'')\longrightarrow\operatorname{Ext}^1_R(M,N').HomR​(M,N′′)⟶ExtR1​(M,N′).

The image of a map M→N′′M\to N''M→N′′ is the obstruction to lifting it to NNN. Analogous sequences exist in the first variable, with its order reversed.

The group Ext⁡R1(M,N)\operatorname{Ext}^1_R(M,N)ExtR1​(M,N) classifies short exact sequences 0→N→E→M→00\to N\to E\to M\to00→N→E→M→0, with equivalence fixing both outer modules and with the split sequence representing zero. Classifying only the abstract middle modules loses information. Finally, MMM is projective exactly when Ext⁡R1(M,N)=0\operatorname{Ext}^1_R(M,N)=0ExtR1​(M,N)=0 for every module NNN; the quantifier is essential.
