曲率张量

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

The Riemann curvature tensor measures the failure of covariant derivatives for the Levi–Civita connection to commute after correcting for the Lie bracket.

Chinese translation, usage & terminology

Prerequisite concepts: Riemannian Metric · Levi–Civita Connection · smooth vector field

Overview

Curvature is an intrinsic measurement of a Riemannian metric, independent of how the manifold is drawn or embedded. Its basic form is the Riemann curvature tensor. Sectional, Ricci, and scalar curvature extract different information from that tensor. This article centers on the full tensor, because a single number cannot generally describe curvature in every tangent direction.

Prerequisites

Use the Levi–Civita connection, the Lie bracket of vector fields, and multilinear algebra. A connection differentiates vector fields but is not itself a tensor in both arguments. Understanding this distinction helps explain why curvature needs a correction term and why Christoffel symbols alone cannot measure intrinsic bending.

Intuition

Differentiating first in one direction and then in another need not produce the same result in reverse order. Even scalar derivatives have a commutator, represented by [X,Y][X,Y][X,Y]. Subtracting its covariant derivative isolates the geometric discrepancy. Infinitesimal parallel transport around a small loop offers another interpretation of this discrepancy; finite-loop transport additionally contains global information.

Important properties

Tensoriality proof idea: expand R(X,Y)(fZ)R(X,Y)(fZ)R(X,Y)(fZ) using the connection's product rule. Terms involving first derivatives of fff cancel, and the remaining second-derivative contribution is (XYf−YXf−[X,Y]f)Z=0(XYf-YXf-[X,Y]f)Z=0(XYf−YXf−[X,Y]f)Z=0. The same calculation in the other slots proves their smooth-function linearity.

The four-tensor is antisymmetric in its first pair and last pair, and unchanged by exchanging the two pairs. Since the Levi–Civita connection has zero torsion, it also satisfies the first Bianchi identity

R(X,Y)Z+R(Y,Z)X+R(Z,X)Y=0.R(X,Y)Z+R(Y,Z)X+R(Z,X)Y=0.R(X,Y)Z+R(Y,Z)X+R(Z,X)Y=0.

Metric compatibility and zero torsion supply different parts of these symmetries; arbitrary connections need not satisfy the entire list.

Ricci curvature contracts the tensor: for an orthonormal basis (ei)(e_i)(ei​), Ric⁡(u,v)=∑ig(R(ei,u)v,ei)\operatorname{Ric}(u,v)=\sum_i g(R(e_i,u)v,e_i)Ric(u,v)=∑i​g(R(ei​,u)v,ei​). Scalar curvature is its metric trace. On a surface scalar curvature is 2K2K2K. Traces discard information, so zero scalar curvature alone is weaker than zero full curvature.

Normal coordinates make the Christoffel symbols zero at one chosen point. Their derivatives can remain nonzero there, and those derivatives contribute to curvature. Flattening first-order coordinate data is therefore compatible with a curved metric.

Connections and further reading

Curvature governs geodesic variation and extends to connections on arbitrary vector bundles. Read Meinrenken, §18 for tensoriality and symmetries, and Wang, Chapter 4 for the sectional-curvature formulation.
