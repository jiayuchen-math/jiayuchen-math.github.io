The Yoneda lemma identifies transformations from a representable presheaf with one element of the target presheaf.

Chinese translation, usage & terminology

Prerequisite concepts: Natural Transformation · Representable Functor · opposite category

Reading routes: Categorical language toward higher structures

Overview

The Yoneda lemma says that a natural family of operations defined on all maps into one object is determined by its value on that object’s identity map. It makes precise the idea of understanding an object through the ways other objects map to it. The statement is abstract, but its proof is a direct calculation with naturality.

Prerequisites

Know locally small categories and contravariant set-valued functors. Such a functor P:Cop→SetP:\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Set}P:Cop→Set is a presheaf. For a small C\mathcal CC, its presheaves form an ordinary locally small functor category. Broader size settings require a fixed universe or explicit control of the transformation collections.

Definition and conventions

For an object AAA of a small category C\mathcal CC and a presheaf PPP, write hA=Hom⁡C(−,A)h_A=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(-,A)hA​=HomC​(−,A). Yoneda gives a bijection, natural in AAA and PPP,

Nat⁡(hA,P)≅P(A),η⟼ηA(1A).\operatorname{Nat}(h_A,P)\cong P(A), \qquad \eta\longmapsto\eta_A(1_A).Nat(hA​,P)≅P(A),η⟼ηA​(1A​).

This is the contravariant formulation. A dual formulation applies to covariant functors using maps out of AAA. Keeping these variances explicit avoids reversing the formula by accident.

Intuition

The identity 1A1_A1A​ is a universal input. Every map f:X→Af:X\to Af:X→A is obtained from it by precomposition. If the operation respects all precompositions, its value at fff is forced by its value at the identity. Thus a huge family can be encoded by a single element.

Examples and non-examples

Let C\mathcal CC be the ordered category {0<1<2}\{0<1<2\}{0<1<2}, with one arrow precisely when the order permits it. Then h1h_1h1​ takes singleton values at zero and one, and the empty value at two. Take PPP to be the constant two-element presheaf with every restriction map the identity.

A natural transformation h1→Ph_1\to Ph1​→P must choose the same value at zero and one because of the arrow 0→10\to10→1. Its component at two is the unique empty function. There are exactly two transformations, corresponding to P(1)P(1)P(1).

Choosing zero at object zero and one at object one gives correctly typed component functions, but violates naturality. It is a non-example of a transformation, not an extra solution missed by the lemma.

Important properties

Given u∈P(A)u\in P(A)u∈P(A), define ηX(f)=P(f)(u)\eta_X(f)=P(f)(u)ηX​(f)=P(f)(u). Contravariant functoriality proves naturality. Conversely, naturality at f:X→Af:X\to Af:X→A forces exactly that formula from ηA(1A)\eta_A(1_A)ηA​(1A​); the two constructions are inverse.

Taking P=hBP=h_BP=hB​ shows that natural transformations hA→hBh_A\to h_BhA​→hB​ correspond to arrows A→BA\to BA→B. Hence the Yoneda embedding is fully faithful. A natural isomorphism of these representable functors forces AAA and BBB to be isomorphic, not necessarily equal.

Connections and further reading

Representability is the existence of a universal element whose induced transformation is invertible. Limits, moduli problems, and many universal constructions can be studied through this language. See Leinster, Theorem 4.2.1 and Corollary 4.3.7 and Li, “可表函子,” theorem labelled prop:Yoneda-lemma.
