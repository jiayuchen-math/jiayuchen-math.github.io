Poincaré 对偶

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

On a closed oriented manifold, cap product with its fundamental class identifies cohomology with homology in complementary dimensions.

Chinese translation, usage & terminology

Prerequisite concepts: Singular Homology · singular cohomology · orientation of a smooth manifold · closed manifold

Overview

Poincaré duality pairs the topology of an oriented manifold in complementary dimensions. A degree-kkk cohomology class determines an (n−k)(n-k)(n−k)-dimensional homology class by cap product with the fundamental class. This is a specific isomorphism, not merely an equality of Betti numbers. Its formulation also explains why compactness, orientation, and the absence of a boundary must be checked.

Prerequisites

Use singular homology and cohomology, the cup product, and relative groups. An orientation specifies compatible generators of the local top-dimensional homology groups. For a closed oriented manifold those local choices assemble into a fundamental class. Here “closed” means compact and without boundary, not closed as a subset of an ambient space.

Definition and conventions

Let MMM be a connected closed oriented topological nnn-manifold, Hausdorff and second countable. Choose its integral fundamental class [M]∈Hn(M;Z)[M]\in H_n(M;\mathbb Z)[M]∈Hn​(M;Z). Then

is an isomorphism for every kkk. We place the homology argument first in the cap product. Reversing orientation negates [M][M][M] and the duality map.

The theorem also works over a commutative coefficient ring when the manifold has a compatible orientation over that ring. In particular, mod-222 duality requires no integral orientation. The integral statement remains an isomorphism of abelian groups, including torsion; replacing it by a pairing of real vector spaces loses that information.

Intuition

A cohomology class tests cycles. Its dual homology class can often be pictured as a complementary-dimensional locus that cycles intersect. This geometric picture requires appropriate representatives and transversality; the cap-product definition supplies a general construction even when no convenient embedded representative has been chosen.

Dimension counting alone is insufficient. The orientation supplies signed intersection data, and the fundamental class tells the construction which global top-dimensional cycle to use.

Important properties

Over a field, cup product followed by evaluation on [M][M][M] gives a nondegenerate pairing between degrees kkk and n−kn-kn−k. Consequently the corresponding Betti numbers agree. On a smooth oriented closed manifold, de Rham theory expresses the real pairing as ([ω],[η])↦∫Mω∧η([\omega],[\eta])\mapsto\int_M\omega\wedge\eta([ω],[η])↦∫M​ω∧η. Stokes' theorem makes it independent of representatives.

For an oriented manifold without boundary that may be noncompact, the appropriate statement is Hck(M;Z)≅Hn−k(M;Z)H_c^k(M;\mathbb Z)\cong H_{n-k}(M;\mathbb Z)Hck​(M;Z)≅Hn−k​(M;Z), using compact supports. For a compact oriented manifold with boundary, the two Lefschetz forms are

Hk(M;Z)≅Hn−k(M,∂M;Z),Hk(M,∂M;Z)≅Hn−k(M;Z).H^k(M;\mathbb Z)\cong H_{n-k}(M,\partial M;\mathbb Z),\qquad H^k(M,\partial M;\mathbb Z)\cong H_{n-k}(M;\mathbb Z).Hk(M;Z)≅Hn−k​(M,∂M;Z),Hk(M,∂M;Z)≅Hn−k​(M;Z).

Proof sketch: first prove compact-support duality in Euclidean coordinate neighborhoods. Compatibility of cap product with Mayer–Vietoris sequences lets the five-lemma glue these local isomorphisms. An increasing-union argument passes to a whole manifold. Compactness then identifies compactly supported cohomology with ordinary cohomology.
