自然变换

Category Theory · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

A natural transformation compares two functors by components commuting with every source morphism.

Chinese translation, usage & terminology

Prerequisite concepts: Functor

Reading routes: Categorical language toward higher structures

Overview

A natural transformation is a map between functors. Its components compare the outputs for individual objects, but those comparisons must also respect every map between the inputs. Naturality converts an informal assertion such as “this construction is compatible with change of input” into a testable equation.

Prerequisites

Understand covariant functors and composition of arrows. The two functors must have a common source and a common target. A family of maps between their values has the right shape to be a transformation, but shape alone does not guarantee naturality.

Definition and conventions

For F,G:C→DF,G:\mathcal C\to\mathcal DF,G:C→D, a natural transformation η:F⇒G\eta:F\Rightarrow Gη:F⇒G consists of arrows ηX:F(X)→G(X)\eta_X:F(X)\to G(X)ηX​:F(X)→G(X) for every object XXX such that, for every f:X→Yf:X\to Yf:X→Y,

G(f)∘ηX=ηY∘F(f).G(f)\circ\eta_X=\eta_Y\circ F(f).G(f)∘ηX​=ηY​∘F(f).

The subscript XXX names a component. The double arrow records a transformation between functors rather than a morphism between objects of C\mathcal CC. If every component is invertible, η\etaη is a natural isomorphism. Componentwise invertibility without the naturality equation is insufficient.

Intuition

There are two routes from F(X)F(X)F(X) to G(Y)G(Y)G(Y). One first compares the constructions at XXX and then transports along fff; the other first transports inside FFF and then compares at YYY. Naturality says the routes agree, so the comparison does not depend on which route a calculation takes.

Examples and non-examples

On sets, let F(A)=A×AF(A)=A\times AF(A)=A×A and let GGG be the identity functor. First-coordinate projections pA(a,b)=ap_A(a,b)=apA​(a,b)=a form a natural transformation. Indeed, for any f:A→Bf:A\to Bf:A→B, both f∘pAf\circ p_Af∘pA​ and pB∘(f×f)p_B\circ(f\times f)pB​∘(f×f) send (a,b)(a,b)(a,b) to f(a)f(a)f(a).

For a non-example, use the category whose objects are sets of size two and whose morphisms are all functions between them. Choose one distinguished element aAa_AaA​ in each object and define ηA\eta_AηA​ to be the constant map with that value. This is a family of endomorphisms of the identity functor. At a two-element set AAA, the swap map sss gives sηAs\eta_AsηA​ constant at s(aA)s(a_A)s(aA​) but ηAs\eta_AsηA​s constant at aAa_AaA​. The maps differ, so the family is not natural.

Important properties

Natural transformations compose vertically by composing components. If η:F⇒G\eta:F\Rightarrow Gη:F⇒G and θ:G⇒H\theta:G\Rightarrow Hθ:G⇒H, then (θη)X=θXηX(\theta\eta)_X=\theta_X\eta_X(θη)X​=θX​ηX​. Substituting the two naturality equations proves naturality of the composite.

If η\etaη is natural and its components invertible, rearranging G(f)ηX=ηYF(f)G(f)\eta_X=\eta_YF(f)G(f)ηX​=ηY​F(f) gives F(f)ηX−1=ηY−1G(f)F(f)\eta_X^{-1}=\eta_Y^{-1}G(f)F(f)ηX−1​=ηY−1​G(f). Thus the inverse components are automatically natural. This is the proof that a componentwise isomorphism is an isomorphism in the functor category.

Connections and further reading

Natural transformations are morphisms in functor categories and supply the unit and counit of adjunctions. Yoneda shows that some transformations are determined by just one element at one object. Read Riehl, §1.4, Definition 1.4.1 and Li, the natural-transformation definition in “函子与自然变换”.
