Sobolev空间

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

A Sobolev space controls a function and its weak derivatives in specified Lebesgue norms.

Chinese translation, usage & terminology

Prerequisite concepts: Lebesgue Lp Space · Weak Derivative · Distribution in Analysis

Reading routes: Integration, Fourier methods and weak solutions

Overview

Sobolev spaces describe smoothness through integrability of weak derivatives. They include functions with corners and other defects that prevent classical differentiation, while retaining enough derivative information for energy estimates. This makes them central to modern analysis and partial differential equations.

The parameters record two independent choices: how many derivatives are controlled and how strongly their size is measured. Domain geometry becomes important when regularity is converted into continuity or boundary values.

Intuition

A weak derivative tests variation after averaging against smooth probes. The norm then quantifies how much variation is present. This permits local irregularities that cost finite energy, while excluding defects such as jumps whose derivative is a point mass rather than an integrable function.

Important properties

Sobolev spaces are Banach, and HkH^kHk is Hilbert. For completeness, take limits of a Cauchy sequence's derivative components in LpL^pLp and pass to the defining integration identities; the limits remain weak derivatives of the limiting function.

Embedding theorems translate derivative integrability into improved integrability or continuity. For example, when p>np>np>n, first-order Sobolev control gives local Hölder representatives, with global versions needing domain assumptions. Trace and compact embedding theorems likewise require explicit geometric and exponent conditions; they are not consequences of notation alone.
