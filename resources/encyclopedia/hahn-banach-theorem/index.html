Hahn-Banach定理

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

A bounded scalar linear functional on a subspace of a normed space extends to the whole space without increasing its norm.

Chinese translation, usage & terminology

Prerequisite concepts: normed vector space · continuous dual space · bounded linear operator

Overview

The Hahn–Banach extension theorem says that a bounded scalar linear measurement defined on part of a normed vector space can be continued to the whole space at no additional norm cost. It explains why continuous dual spaces contain enough functionals to detect vectors and recover their norms.

There are several related theorems under the same name, including domination and geometric separation forms. The main statement here is the norm-preserving extension form for real or complex normed spaces. Completeness of the space and closedness of the initial subspace are unnecessary.

Prerequisites

Know normed spaces, linear subspaces, and the continuous dual X∗X^*X∗. For a bounded functional f:M→Ff:M\to\mathbb Ff:M→F, where F=R\mathbb F=\mathbb RF=R or C\mathbb CC, its norm is sup⁡∥m∥≤1∣f(m)∣\sup_{\|m\|\le1}|f(m)|sup∥m∥≤1​∣f(m)∣, with the norm inherited from XXX. Boundedness is equivalent to continuity for linear maps. The zero functional on the zero subspace has norm zero.

Definition and conventions

Let MMM be any linear subspace of a normed space XXX over F\mathbb FF, and let f∈M∗f\in M^*f∈M∗. There exists F∈X∗F\in X^*F∈X∗ such that

F∣M=f,∥F∥X∗=∥f∥M∗.F|_M=f,\qquad \|F\|_{X^*}=\|f\|_{M^*}.F∣M​=f,∥F∥X∗​=∥f∥M∗​.

The extension respects the chosen scalar field; in a complex space it is complex-linear. The theorem asserts existence, not uniqueness or a canonical extension rule. It concerns scalar-valued functionals and does not assert the same norm-preserving extension property for arbitrary maps into Banach spaces.

An underlying real version states that if p:X→Rp:X\to\mathbb Rp:X→R is sublinear and f≤pf\le pf≤p on MMM, then a real linear extension exists with F≤pF\le pF≤p on XXX. Sublinear means subadditive and positively homogeneous. Taking p(x)=∥f∥∥x∥p(x)=\|f\|\|x\|p(x)=∥f∥∥x∥ produces the norm form.

Intuition

The norm bound constrains the new value assigned when one extra direction is added. Subadditivity ensures that all resulting upper and lower constraints are compatible. The surprising part is that this compatibility persists across the whole vector space, even without an inner product or orthogonal projection.
