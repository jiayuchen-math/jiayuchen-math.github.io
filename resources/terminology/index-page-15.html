pointwise convergence 点点收敛

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A sequence of real functions f_n on E converges pointwise to f if for each fixed x in E the scalar sequence f_n(x) converges to f(x).

Poisson distribution 泊松分布

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For $\lambda>0$, a law on nonnegative integers with $P(X=k)=e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k/k!$.

Poisson equation Poisson方程

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The Poisson equation is an inhomogeneous Laplace equation -Delta u=f on an open set, with prescribed source f; using Delta u=f instead changes the sign convention for the source.

Poisson process 泊松过程

Probability · Undergraduate

For rate $\lambda>0$, a right-continuous counting process $N_t$ starting at zero with stationary independent increments and $N_t-N_s\sim\operatorname{Pois}(\lambda(t-s))$ for $s<t$.

Encyclopedia

pole of a complex function 极点

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An isolated singularity a of f is a pole of order m>=1 if f(z)=g(z)/(z-a)^m near a with g holomorphic and g(a) nonzero.

polynomial ring 多项式环

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a commutative ring $R$, the ring $R[x]$ of finite formal sums $\sum a_ix^i$ with coefficientwise addition and convolution multiplication.

power series in analysis 幂级数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A power series centered at a is a series sum_{n>=0} c_n(z-a)^n with real or complex coefficients, studied as a function on the points where its partial sums converge.

power set 幂集

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The set of every subset of a given set A.

preimage of a subset under a function 原像

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For f:A→B and T⊆B, the members a of A such that f(a) belongs to T.

prime element 素元

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A nonzero nonunit $p$ in a domain such that $p\mid ab$ implies $p\mid a$ or $p\mid b$.

prime ideal 素理想

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A proper ideal $\mathfrak p$ in a commutative ring with $ab\in\mathfrak p$ implying $a\in\mathfrak p$ or $b\in\mathfrak p$.

prime number 素数

Number Theory · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A positive integer $p>1$ whose only positive divisors are $1$ and $p$.

prime spectrum of a commutative ring 交换环的素谱空间

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate

The set of prime ideals of $R$ with closed sets $V(I)=\{\mathfrak p:I\subseteq\mathfrak p\}$.

Encyclopedia

primitive Dirichlet character 本原狄利克雷特征

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

A Dirichlet character modulo $N$ that is not induced from a character modulo any proper divisor of $N$.

principal ideal domain 主理想环

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A domain whose every ideal is generated by a single element.

probabilistic method 概率方法

Combinatorics · Undergraduate

The probabilistic method proves existence of a combinatorial object by defining a distribution on candidates and showing that the desired property has positive probability, sometimes after a controlled alteration.

Encyclopedia

probability density function 概率密度

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A nonnegative measurable function $f$ with $P(X\in B)=\int_B f(x)\,dx$ for every Borel set $B$, when the law is absolutely continuous with respect to Lebesgue measure.

probability distribution 概率分布

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The probability measure induced by a random variable $X$, given by $\mu_X(B)=P(X\in B)$ on Borel sets.

probability mass function 概率质量函数

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a discrete random variable with countable support $S$, the function $p(x)=P(X=x)$ on $S$, whose nonnegative values sum to one.

probability space 概率空间

Probability · Undergraduate

A triple $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ in which $\mathcal F$ is a sigma-algebra on $\Omega$ and $P$ is a countably additive nonnegative measure with $P(\Omega)=1$.

Encyclopedia

product topology 乘积空间

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A Cartesian product equipped with the coarsest topology making every coordinate projection continuous; basic opens restrict only finitely many coordinates.

projective module 投射模

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate · Short definition only

A module $P$ such that every module map $P\to C$ lifts through every surjective module map $B\to C$.

proof by contradiction 反证法

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate

In classical logic, a proof of $P$ obtained by deriving $\bot$ from the premises together with $\neg P$.

Encyclopedia

proof by contraposition 逆否法证明

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A classical proof of $P\to Q$ by deriving $\neg P$ from $\neg Q$.

pseudoholomorphic curve 伪全纯曲线

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Short definition only

A smooth map from a Riemann surface with complex structure j to a smooth manifold with almost complex structure J whose differential intertwines those structures, so du composed with j equals J composed with du.
