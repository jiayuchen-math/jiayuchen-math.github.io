波前集

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Research · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a distribution u, its smooth wavefront set consists of pairs (x,xi) with xi nonzero for which no smooth compactly supported cutoff equal to one near x has a Fourier transform of its product with u decaying faster than every inverse power in a conic neighborhood of xi.

分布的光滑波前集由点与非零余向量方向的配对组成；若任何在该点附近为一的光滑局部截断，都不能使其傅立叶变换在此方向的锥邻域中快速衰减，则该配对属于波前集。

Usage and distinctions

Singular support records positions only; the wavefront set also records covector directions and excludes the zero covector.
