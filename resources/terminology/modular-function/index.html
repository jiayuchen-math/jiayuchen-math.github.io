Other names / variants: meromorphic modular function

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a congruence subgroup Γ\GammaΓ, a meromorphic function on the upper half-plane invariant under Γ\GammaΓ and meromorphic at all cusps, equivalently a meromorphic weight-zero modular form.

对同余子群 Γ\GammaΓ，在上半平面亚纯、对 Γ\GammaΓ 不变、且在所有尖点也亚纯的函数；即权为零的亚纯模形式。

Usage and distinctions

A pole at a cusp is allowed; a holomorphic weight-zero modular form for a congruence subgroup is constant.
