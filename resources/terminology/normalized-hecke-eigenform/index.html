正规化 Hecke 特征形式

Number Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: normalized eigenform

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A nonzero modular form on Γ1(N)\Gamma_1(N)Γ1​(N) that is a simultaneous eigenvector for its Hecke algebra and has Fourier coefficient a1=1a_1=1a1​=1 at infinity.

Γ1(N)\Gamma_1(N)Γ1​(N) 上对其 Hecke 代数为共同特征向量、且在无穷尖点的傅里叶系数满足 a1=1a_1=1a1​=1 的非零模形式。

Example

Δ=q−24q2+252q3+⋯\Delta=q-24q^2+252q^3+\cdotsΔ=q−24q2+252q3+⋯, with q=e2πiτq=e^{2\pi i\tau}q=e2πiτ, is normalized and satisfies T2Δ=−24ΔT_2\Delta=-24\DeltaT2​Δ=−24Δ.

Usage and distinctions

Normalization fixes scalar ambiguity but does not assert that the form is new at its level; a general eigenform need not be a cusp form.
