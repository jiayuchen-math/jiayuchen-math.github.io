加法原理

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: sum rule

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For finitely many pairwise disjoint finite sets, the cardinality of their union equals the sum of their cardinalities.

有限个两两不交的有限集合，其并集的元素个数等于各集合元素个数之和。

Example

There are 3+4=73+4=73+4=7 symbols when choosing one symbol from disjoint alphabets of sizes 3 and 4.

Usage and distinctions

Cases must be both exhaustive and disjoint; overlapping cases require a correction.
