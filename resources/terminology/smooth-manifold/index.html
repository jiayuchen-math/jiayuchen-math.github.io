微分流形

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A Hausdorff, second countable space locally homeomorphic to open subsets of a fixed Euclidean space, together with a smooth structure represented by charts whose transition maps are infinitely differentiable; boundary is excluded here.

具有 Hausdorff 性和第二可数性、局部同胚于固定维数欧氏空间开集，并配有坐标变换无穷次可微的微分结构的空间；本条不含边界。

Example

The unit sphere S2S^2S2 is smooth: near a point with z≠0z\ne0z=0, solve z=±1−x2−y2z=\pm\sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}z=±1−x2−y2​ to obtain local coordinates.

Usage and distinctions

Local Euclidean topology alone does not choose a smooth structure. The union of two crossing coordinate axes fails even the manifold condition at the crossing.
