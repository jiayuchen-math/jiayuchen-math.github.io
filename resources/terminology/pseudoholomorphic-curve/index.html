伪全纯曲线

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A smooth map from a Riemann surface with complex structure j to a smooth manifold with almost complex structure J whose differential intertwines those structures, so du composed with j equals J composed with du.

从复结构为 jjj 的黎曼曲面到近复结构为 JJJ 的光滑流形的光滑映射 uuu，满足 du∘j=J∘dudu\circ j=J\circ dudu∘j=J∘du。

Example

For the usual structures on C\mathbb CC, u(z)=z2u(z)=z^2u(z)=z2 is pseudoholomorphic; u(z)=z‾u(z)=\overline zu(z)=z is not, since its differential anticommutes with multiplication by iii.

Usage and distinctions

The word curve denotes a parametrized map, not just its image. The map need not be embedded or immersive; branch points and constant maps are allowed by this definition.
