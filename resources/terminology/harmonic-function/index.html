调和函数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A real or complex C^2 function u on an open subset of R^n is harmonic when its Laplacian, the sum of the coordinate second derivatives, vanishes throughout the domain.

欧氏空间开集上的二阶连续可微函数，其各坐标二阶偏导数之和处处为零时，称为调和函数。

Example

u(x,y)=3x2−3y2u(x,y)=3x^2-3y^2u(x,y)=3x2−3y2 is harmonic because uxx+uyy=6−6=0u_{xx}+u_{yy}=6-6=0uxx​+uyy​=6−6=0.

Usage and distinctions

A harmonic function is a solution of the homogeneous Laplace equation; a nonzero source gives a Poisson equation.
