variance 方差

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For $E[X^2]<\infty$, the variance is $\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[(X-E[X])^2]$.

vector space 向量空间

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate

An abelian group $V$ with a unital scalar action of a field $F$, distributive in both inputs and satisfying $(ab)v=a(bv)$.

Encyclopedia

vertex cover 顶点覆盖

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A vertex cover of a simple graph G is a vertex subset C such that every edge of G has at least one endpoint in C.

vertex degree 顶点度

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

In a finite simple graph, the degree d(v) of a vertex v is the number of its neighbors, equivalently the number of edges incident with v.

wandering point 游荡点

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a continuous self-map $T$, a point having a neighborhood $U$ such that $T^n(U)\cap U=\varnothing$ for every integer $n\ge1$.

wave equation 波方程

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The constant-speed wave equation is partial_t^2 u=c^2 Delta_x u with c>0, classically for a twice differentiable space-time function; an initial value problem normally specifies both u and partial_t u at time zero.

weak convergence in a normed space 弱收敛

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate · Short definition only

A sequence x_n in a normed space converges weakly to x if lambda(x_n) tends to lambda(x) for every bounded linear scalar functional lambda.

Dynamical Systems · Graduate · Short definition only

A probability-preserving map is weakly mixing if $N^{-1}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}|\mu(A\cap T^{-n}B)-\mu(A)\mu(B)|\to0$ for every measurable $A,B$.

well-order 良序

Foundations & Logic · Graduate · Short definition only

A total order on a set in which every nonempty subset has a least element.

Yoneda lemma 米田引理

Category Theory · Graduate

For small $C$, presheaf $P$, and $A\in C$, evaluation at $1_A$ gives $\operatorname{Nat}(\operatorname{Hom}(-,A),P)\cong P(A)$ naturally.

zero divisor 零因子

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A nonzero element of a commutative ring whose product with some nonzero element is zero.
