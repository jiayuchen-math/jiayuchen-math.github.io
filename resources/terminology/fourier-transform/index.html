傅立叶变换

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For f in L1(R^n), the Fourier transform in the convention used here is the function f-hat(xi)=integral f(x)e^{-i x dot xi} dx.

对 R^n 上可积函数，此处把积分 f(x)e^{-ix·ξ} 定义为傅立叶变换；采用这一约定时，逆变换需乘 (2π)^{-n}。

Example

For f(x)=e−2x1[0,∞)(x)f(x)=e^{-2x}\mathbf1_{[0,\infty)}(x)f(x)=e−2x1[0,∞)​(x), f^(ξ)=1/(2+iξ)\widehat f(\xi)=1/(2+i\xi)f​(ξ)=1/(2+iξ) by integrating the exponential.

Usage and distinctions

The transform has a continuous frequency variable; a periodic Fourier series has integer-indexed coefficients. Normalization constants vary by source.
