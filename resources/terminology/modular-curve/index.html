Meaning in context

For a congruence subgroup Γ\GammaΓ, let H={τ∈C:Im⁡τ>0}\mathcal H=\{\tau\in\mathbb C:\operatorname{Im}\tau>0\}H={τ∈C:Imτ>0}. The modular curves are the Riemann surface Y(Γ)=Γ\HY(\Gamma)=\Gamma\backslash\mathcal HY(Γ)=Γ\H and its compactification X(Γ)X(\Gamma)X(Γ) by adjoining Γ\P1(Q)\Gamma\backslash\mathbb P^1(\mathbb Q)Γ\P1(Q); specify Y or X to distinguish them.

对同余子群 Γ\GammaΓ，令 H={τ∈C:Im⁡τ>0}\mathcal H=\{\tau\in\mathbb C:\operatorname{Im}\tau>0\}H={τ∈C:Imτ>0}。模曲线指商黎曼面 Y(Γ)=Γ\HY(\Gamma)=\Gamma\backslash\mathcal HY(Γ)=Γ\H 以及添入尖点类 Γ\P1(Q)\Gamma\backslash\mathbb P^1(\mathbb Q)Γ\P1(Q) 后的紧化 X(Γ)X(\Gamma)X(Γ)；须用 YYY 或 XXX 区分。

Usage and distinctions

The quotient has a Riemann surface structure even at elliptic orbits; it is not a smooth free quotient everywhere. This entry fixes the classical complex-analytic sense.
