cyclotomic polynomial 分圆多项式

Number Theory · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For $n\ge1$, $\Phi_n(X)$ is the monic polynomial whose roots are the complex roots of unity of exact order $n$.

de Rham cohomology de Rham 上同调

Geometry & Topology · Graduate

For a smooth manifold, the real vector spaces obtained in each degree by dividing the closed smooth differential forms by the exact ones, using the exterior derivative; degree zero has no incoming differential.

Encyclopedia

deck transformation 复迭变换

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A homeomorphism of a covering's total space E satisfying p composed with that homeomorphism equals p.

Dedekind zeta function 戴德金 ζ 函数

Number Theory · Graduate

For a number field $K$, $\zeta_K(s)=\sum_{0\ne\mathfrak a\subseteq\mathcal O_K}|\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak a|^{-s}$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>1$, with its meromorphic continuation.

Encyclopedia

deformation retract 强形变收缩

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A subspace A of X for which a homotopy of the identity of X to a retraction onto A fixes every point of A at every time.

dense subset 稠密

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A subset whose closure equals the entire ambient topological space.

derangement 错排

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A derangement of {1,…,n} is a bijection σ of that set with σ(i) ≠ i for every i. The empty permutation is a derangement by the vacuous convention.

derivative of a real function 导数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate

For a real function defined near a, its derivative at a is the finite limit of (f(a+h)-f(a))/h as nonzero h tends to zero.

Encyclopedia

Devaney chaos Devaney混沌

Dynamical Systems · Graduate

For a continuous self-map of an infinite compact metric space without isolated points, topological transitivity together with density of periodic points; these hypotheses imply sensitive dependence.

Encyclopedia

diagonalizable linear map 可对角化线性映射

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate

A finite-dimensional endomorphism possessing a basis of eigenvectors over the specified scalar field.

Encyclopedia

differential form 外微分形式

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate

For a smooth manifold and a nonnegative integer k, a smooth section of the kth exterior power of its cotangent bundle; pointwise it is an alternating real multilinear function of k tangent vectors.

Encyclopedia

differential graded category dg-范畴

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Research

A category enriched in cochain complexes over a commutative ring, with associative unital degree-zero composition that is a map of complexes.

Encyclopedia

differential of a real function 微分

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a function differentiable at a, its differential is the linear map h mapped to f'(a)h in f(a+h)=f(a)+f'(a)h+o(|h|).

dimension of a vector space 向量空间的维数

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The cardinality of any basis of a vector space over its specified field.

direct proof 直接证明

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate

An argument deriving the conclusion from the given hypotheses by justified steps, without adopting its negation as the main temporary assumption.

Encyclopedia

direct sum of modules 模的直和

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate · Short definition only

The module of tuples from a family $(M_i)$ having only finitely many nonzero coordinates, with coordinatewise operations.

directional derivative 方向导数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For f on an open subset of R^n and a specified unit vector v, the directional derivative at a is the finite two-sided limit [f(a+tv)-f(a)]/t as t tends to zero, when it exists.

Dirichlet character 狄利克雷特征

Number Theory · Graduate

For $N\ge1$, a homomorphism $(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times\to\mathbb C^\times$, extended periodically to integers and assigned $0$ on integers not coprime to $N$.

Encyclopedia

Dirichlet L-function 狄利克雷 L 函数

Number Theory · Graduate

For a Dirichlet character $\chi$, $L(s,\chi)=\sum_{n\ge1}\chi(n)n^{-s}$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>1$, with its meromorphic continuation.

Encyclopedia

Dirichlet's unit theorem 狄利克雷单位定理

Number Theory · Graduate

If a number field has $r_1$ real embeddings and $r_2$ conjugate pairs of complex embeddings, then $\mathcal O_K^\times\cong\mu_K\times\mathbb Z^{r_1+r_2-1}$, where $\mu_K$ is its group of roots of unity.

Encyclopedia

discrete topology 离散拓扑

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The topology on a set in which every subset is open.

discriminant of a number field 数域的判别式

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For an integral basis $\omega_1,\ldots,\omega_n$ of $K$, $D_K=\det(\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(\omega_i\omega_j))$, independent of that basis.

disjoint union 无交并

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For indexed sets, the set of pairs (i,x) with x in the i-th set, retaining the component index.

disjunction 析取

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The inclusive connective producing a statement true when at least one component is true.

distribution in analysis 广义函数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate

A distribution on an open set Omega in R^n is a continuous linear functional on C_c^infinity(Omega); locally, its action is bounded by finitely many derivative suprema of tests with support in a fixed compact set.
