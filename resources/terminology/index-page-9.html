The constant-coefficient heat equation is partial_t u=kappa Delta_x u with kappa>0, for a space-time function u that is once time differentiable and twice space differentiable in the classical formulation.

Hensel's lemma 亨泽尔引理

Number Theory · Graduate

For prime $p$ and $f\in\mathbb Z_p[X]$, a simple root $a$ of $f\bmod p$ lifts to a unique root $\alpha\in\mathbb Z_p$ with $\alpha\equiv a\pmod p$.

higher category 高阶范畴

Category Theory · Research

A categorical structure with higher morphisms and coherent composition; the scoped (∞,1) model here is a simplicial set admitting fillers for every inner horn.

higher homotopy group 同伦群

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Short definition only

For n at least two, the abelian group of based maps from S^n to a based space modulo based homotopy, with addition from pinching the sphere into a wedge of two spheres.

Hilbert class field 希尔伯特类域

Number Theory · Graduate

For a number field $K$, its maximal abelian extension unramified at every finite place and split at every real place.

Encyclopedia

Hilbert space Hilbert空间

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate

A Hilbert space is a real or complex inner-product space complete for the norm $\sqrt{\langle x,x\rangle}$ induced by that inner product.

holomorphic cusp form 尖点形式

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

A holomorphic modular form on a congruence subgroup whose Fourier expansion at each cusp has zero constant term.

A complex-valued function on an open subset of C is holomorphic when its complex difference quotient has a finite limit at every point, with the increment approaching zero through all complex directions.

homeomorphism 同胚

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A bijective continuous map of topological spaces whose inverse is also continuous.

homology of a module complex 模复形的同调

For a chain complex $C$, the module $H_n(C)=\ker d_n/\operatorname{im}d_{n+1}$.

homotopy 同伦

A continuous map H from X times the unit interval to Y whose endpoint slices are the specified continuous maps X to Y.

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An additive subgroup $I$ of a commutative ring $R$ with $rI\subseteq I$ for every $r\in R$.

idele group of a number field 数域的伊代尔群

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For a number field $K$, the restricted product of $K_v^\times$ relative to $\mathcal O_v^\times$ at finite places; algebraically it is $\mathbb A_K^\times$.

implicit function theorem 隐函数定理

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

If F is a C^1 map near (a,b) in R^n times R^m, F(a,b)=0, and its derivative with respect to the second variable is invertible there, then nearby solutions F(x,y)=0 uniquely have y=g(x) for a C^1 map g with g(a)=b.

inclusion–exclusion principle 容斥原理

Combinatorics · Undergraduate

For finite sets A_1,…,A_m, the size of their union is the sum over nonempty index sets S of (−1)^(|S|+1) times the size of the intersection indexed by S.

Encyclopedia

independent and identically distributed 独立同分布

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A family of random variables that is mutually independent and whose members all have the same probability law.

independent events 独立事件

Probability · Undergraduate

Events $A_i$ are mutually independent if every finite subfamily satisfies $P(\bigcap_{i\in J}A_i)=\prod_{i\in J}P(A_i)$.

Encyclopedia

independent random variables 独立随机变量

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

Variables $X_i$ whose every finite subfamily satisfies $P(X_{i_j}\in B_j,\ 1\le j\le r)=\prod_{j=1}^rP(X_{i_j}\in B_j)$ for all Borel sets $B_j$.

independent set in a graph 独立集

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An independent set in a simple graph is a vertex subset containing no adjacent pair of distinct vertices.
