适应过程

Probability · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: adapted process · 适应列（离散时间）

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

Relative to a filtration (Ft)(\mathcal F_t)(Ft​), a process (Xt)(X_t)(Xt​) is adapted when XtX_tXt​ is Ft\mathcal F_tFt​-measurable at every time ttt.

相对于给定信息流，每个时刻的变量都只使用该时刻已知的可测信息的过程。

Example

The partial sum SnS_nSn​ of the first nnn coin increments is adapted to their natural filtration.

Usage and distinctions

Adaptedness does not alone imply joint measurability or predictability; discrete predictable values are measurable one step earlier.
