Hamilton 矢量场

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a smooth real function H on a symplectic manifold with form omega, the unique smooth vector field X_H satisfying contraction of omega with X_H equals dH; this entry uses the positive-dH sign convention.

对辛流形上的光滑实函数 HHH，以缩并方程 ιXHω=dH\iota_{X_H}\omega=dHιXH​​ω=dH 唯一确定的光滑向量场；本条采用正 dHdHdH 的符号约定。

Example

With ω=dq∧dp\omega=dq\wedge dpω=dq∧dp and H=(q2+p2)/2H=(q^2+p^2)/2H=(q2+p2)/2, XH=p∂q−q∂pX_H=p\partial_q-q\partial_pXH​=p∂q​−q∂p​, giving q˙=p\dot q=pq˙​=p and p˙=−q\dot p=-qp˙​=−q.

Usage and distinctions

Some authors use the opposite sign. A vector field preserving the symplectic form need not be Hamiltonian: contraction must be globally exact, rather than merely closed.
