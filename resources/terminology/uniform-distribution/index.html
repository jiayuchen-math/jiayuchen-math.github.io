均匀分布

Probability · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For real a<ba<ba<b, the law with density 1/(b−a)1/(b-a)1/(b−a) on [a,b][a,b][a,b] and zero elsewhere.

有限非退化区间上的连续分布，密度在该区间内恒为区间长度的倒数，在外为0。

Example

Uniform probability on [2,6][2,6][2,6] assigns probability 1/41/41/4 to [3,4][3,4][3,4].

Usage and distinctions

This record concerns a real interval; a discrete uniform law uses equal masses, and endpoints do not change this continuous law.
