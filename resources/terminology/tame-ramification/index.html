p 进局部域的温分歧

Number Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: tamely ramified local extension

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A finite extension L/KL/KL/K of ppp-adic local fields is tamely ramified when p∤e(L/K)p\nmid e(L/K)p∤e(L/K).

对 ppp 进局部域的有限扩张 L/KL/KL/K，若 ppp 不整除分歧指数，则称其温分歧。

Example

Q3(3)/Q3\mathbb Q_3(\sqrt3)/\mathbb Q_3Q3​(3​)/Q3​ is tamely ramified, with e=2e=2e=2.

Usage and distinctions

Tame includes unramified extensions (e=1); wild ramification has p dividing e in this finite-residue-field setting.
