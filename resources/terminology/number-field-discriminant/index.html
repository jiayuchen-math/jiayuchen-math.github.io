数域的判别式

Number Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: absolute number-field discriminant

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For an integral basis ω1,…,ωn\omega_1,\ldots,\omega_nω1​,…,ωn​ of KKK, DK=det⁡(Tr⁡K/Q(ωiωj))D_K=\det(\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(\omega_i\omega_j))DK​=det(TrK/Q​(ωi​ωj​)), independent of that basis.

若 ω1,…,ωn\omega_1,\ldots,\omega_nω1​,…,ωn​ 是 KKK 的整基，则 DK=det⁡(Tr⁡K/Q(ωiωj))D_K=\det(\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(\omega_i\omega_j))DK​=det(TrK/Q​(ωi​ωj​))，此值与整基选取无关。

Example

DQ(5)=5D_{\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)}=5DQ(5​)​=5.

Usage and distinctions

A power-basis discriminant may differ by the square of an index; for the basis 1, sqrt(5) it is 20.
