open subset 开集

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A subset belonging to the specified topology of a space.

operator norm 线性算子范数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate · Short definition only

For a bounded linear map T between normed spaces, its operator norm is sup{norm(Tx):norm(x)<=1}, equivalently the least constant in its global linear bound.

opposite category 反范畴

Category Theory · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The category with the same objects as C, all arrows reversed, and composition performed in the reverse order.

orbit of a dynamical system 轨道

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate

For a self-map $T$, the forward orbit of $x$ is $\{T^nx:n\ge0\}$; when $T$ is invertible, its full orbit is $\{T^nx:n\in\mathbb Z\}$.

Encyclopedia

orbit of a group action 群作用的轨道

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For $x$ in a $G$-set, the subset $Gx=\{gx:g\in G\}$.

ordinary generating function 普通生成函数

Combinatorics · Undergraduate

For a sequence (a_n) indexed by nonnegative integers in a commutative ring R, its ordinary generating function is the formal series ∑ a_n x^n in R[[x]].

p-adic additive valuation p 进加性赋值

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For prime $p$ and nonzero $x\in\mathbb Q$, write $x=p^e a/b$ with $p\nmid ab$ and define $v_p(x)=e$; set $v_p(0)=+\infty$.

p-adic number field p 进数域

Number Theory · Graduate

For prime $p$, $\mathbb Q_p$ is the completion of $\mathbb Q$ for the absolute value $|x|_p=p^{-v_p(x)}$.

Encyclopedia

pairwise independent events 两两独立事件

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A family of events in one probability space for which $P(A_i\cap A_j)=P(A_i)P(A_j)$ whenever $i\ne j$.

Parseval identity for Fourier series Parseval等式

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a 2 pi-periodic function f in L2 and coefficients c_k=(2 pi)^(-1) integral f(x)e^{-ikx} dx, Parseval's identity states (2 pi)^(-1) integral_{-pi}^pi |f|^2=sum_{k in Z}|c_k|^2.

partial derivative 偏导数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For f on an open subset of R^n, its jth partial derivative at a is the finite limit [f(a+h e_j)-f(a)]/h as nonzero h tends to zero, when that limit exists.

partial order 偏序

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate

A reflexive, antisymmetric and transitive binary relation on a set.

Encyclopedia

path component 道路分支

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An equivalence class under the relation that two points can be joined by a path in the space.

path in a graph 路

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

In a simple graph, a path is a finite sequence of distinct vertices whose consecutive pairs are edges; its length is the number of consecutive pairs.

path-connected space 道路连通

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A space in which every pair of points is joined by a continuous map from the unit interval.

Pell equation 佩尔方程

Number Theory · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a positive nonsquare integer $d$, the equation $x^2-dy^2=1$ with integer unknowns $x,y$.

perfect matching 完美匹配

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A perfect matching in a finite simple graph is a matching incident with every vertex.

periodic point 周期点

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A point $x$ for which $T^rx=x$ for some integer $r\ge1$; its least period is the smallest such positive integer, and the orbit is called periodic.

permutation of a finite set 排列

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A permutation of a finite set X is an ordering of all its elements, equivalently a bijection from {1,…,|X|} to X. An n-element set has n! such orderings, including one empty ordering when n=0.

pigeonhole principle 鸽巢原理

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

If n objects are assigned to m ≥ 1 categories, at least one category contains at least the ceiling of n/m objects; in particular n>m forces a repeated category.

Poincaré duality Poincaré 对偶

Geometry & Topology · Graduate

For a closed oriented n-manifold with chosen integral fundamental class, cap product with that class is an isomorphism from H^k(M;Z) to H_{n-k}(M;Z).

Encyclopedia

Poincaré recurrence theorem 庞加莱回复定理

Dynamical Systems · Graduate

For a probability-preserving transformation $T$ and measurable $A$, almost every $x\in A$ returns to $A$ infinitely often under positive iterates.

Encyclopedia

Poincaré return map 首次返回映射

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a flow and a transverse section, the map taking a point to its next positive-time intersection with that section, on the domain where the first return exists.
