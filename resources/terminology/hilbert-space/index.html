Hilbert空间

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A Hilbert space is a real or complex inner-product space complete for the norm ⟨x,x⟩\sqrt{\langle x,x\rangle}⟨x,x⟩​ induced by that inner product.

Hilbert 空间是由内积诱导的范数下完备的实或复内积空间；复内积须指定共轭线性所在的变量。

Example

ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2 with ⟨x,y⟩=∑xnyn‾\langle x,y\rangle=\sum x_n\overline{y_n}⟨x,y⟩=∑xn​yn​​ is Hilbert; ∥(1,2,0,…)∥=5\|(1,2,0,\ldots)\|=\sqrt5∥(1,2,0,…)∥=5​.

Usage and distinctions

A Banach space needs only a complete norm; a Hilbert norm also obeys the parallelogram identity.
