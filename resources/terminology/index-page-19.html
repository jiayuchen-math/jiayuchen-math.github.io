subgraph 子图

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A graph H=(W,F) is a subgraph of G=(V,E) when W ⊆ V and F ⊆ E, with every edge of F having its endpoints in W.

subgroup 子群

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A subset $H$ of a group $G$ containing the identity and closed under multiplication and inverses.

submartingale 下鞅

Probability · Graduate · Short definition only

An integrable adapted discrete-time process $X_n$ with $E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\ge X_n$ almost surely for every $n$.

submodule 子模

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate · Short definition only

An additive subgroup $N$ of an $R$-module $M$ closed under the $R$-scalar action.

subsequence of a real sequence 子列

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A subsequence of (a_n) is (a_{n_k}) where the positive integer indices n_k increase strictly.

subset 子集

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

$A$ is a subset of $B$ if $x\in A$ implies $x\in B$ for every $x$.

subspace topology 子空间拓扑

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a subset A of a topological space X, the topology on A consisting of intersections of A with open subsets of X.

sumset 和集

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For subsets A,B of an additively written abelian group, their sumset A+B is {a+b:a∈A,b∈B}; each resulting element is included once, regardless of representation multiplicity.

supermartingale 上鞅

Probability · Graduate · Short definition only

An integrable adapted discrete-time process $X_n$ with $E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\le X_n$ almost surely for every $n$.

supremum of a real set 上确界

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a nonempty real set A bounded above, its supremum is the least real upper bound: it bounds every member of A and is no larger than any other upper bound.

surjective function 满射

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A function f:A→B whose image is all of B.

Sylow subgroup Sylow 子群

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a finite group of order $p^am$ with prime $p$ and $p\nmid m$, a subgroup of order $p^a$.

Taylor formula with integral remainder Taylor公式

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For an integer m>=0 and a real C^{m+1} function on an interval containing a and x, Taylor's formula writes f(x)=sum_{k=0}^m f^{(k)}(a)(x-a)^k/k! + (1/m!) integral_a^x (x-t)^m f^{(m+1)}(t) dt.

tempered distribution 缓增分布

A tempered distribution on R^n is a continuous linear functional on Schwartz space with its topology given by all polynomially weighted derivative seminorms.

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate
