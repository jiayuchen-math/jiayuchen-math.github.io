Meaning in context

In an abelian category, a sequence of differential objects (Er,dr)(E_r,d_r)(Er​,dr​) with dr2=0d_r^2=0dr2​=0 and specified isomorphisms H(Er,dr)≅Er+1H(E_r,d_r)\cong E_{r+1}H(Er​,dr​)≅Er+1​; the cohomological bigraded version has differential of bidegree (r,1−r)(r,1-r)(r,1−r).
