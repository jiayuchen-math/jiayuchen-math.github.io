topological dynamical system 拓扑动力系统

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate · Short definition only

In the discrete-time convention, a pair $(X,T)$ with a topological space $X$ and continuous self-map $T:X\to X$, whose evolution is given by the iterates of $T$.

topological embedding 嵌入映射

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A continuous map that is a homeomorphism onto its image equipped with the subspace topology.

topological manifold 拓扑流形

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a fixed nonnegative integer n, a Hausdorff, second countable space in which each point has a neighborhood homeomorphic to an open subset of real n-dimensional Euclidean space; boundary is excluded in this record.

topological mixing 拓扑混合

Dynamical Systems · Graduate · Short definition only

A continuous self-map $T$ is topologically mixing when for every nonempty open $U,V$, some $N$ makes $T^n(U)\cap V\ne\varnothing$ for every $n\ge N$.

topological space 拓扑空间

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate

A pair consisting of a set and a family of subsets containing the empty set and the whole set and closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections.

Encyclopedia

topological transitivity 拓扑传递

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate

A continuous self-map $T$ is topologically transitive if for every nonempty open $U,V$ some $n\ge0$ satisfies $T^n(U)\cap V\ne\varnothing$.

Encyclopedia

total differential 全微分

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For f from an open subset of R^n to R^m, the total differential at a is the linear map A for which f(a+h)-f(a)-Ah=o(|h|), if such A exists.

trace of a number-field element 数域元素的迹

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For a number field $K$ and $\alpha\in K$, $\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(\alpha)$ is the trace of the $\mathbb Q$-linear map $x\mapsto\alpha x$ on $K$.

tree in graph theory 树

Combinatorics · Undergraduate

A tree is a nonempty connected simple graph containing no cycle; this record restricts to finite trees.

Encyclopedia

two-color clique Ramsey number 拉姆齐数

Combinatorics · Undergraduate

For positive integers s,t, R(s,t) is the least positive N such that every red-blue edge coloring of K_N has a red K_s or a blue K_t.

Encyclopedia

uniform boundedness principle 一致有界原理

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate · Short definition only

For a family of bounded linear maps from a Banach space X to a normed space Y, if sup_T norm(Tx) is finite for every x in X, then sup_T norm(T) is finite.

uniform continuity 一致连续

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate

A map f between metric spaces is uniformly continuous if for every epsilon > 0 there is delta > 0 such that d(x,y) < delta implies d(f(x),f(y)) < epsilon for every pair of domain points.

Encyclopedia

uniform convergence 一致收敛

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate

A sequence of real functions f_n on a common set E converges uniformly to f if for every epsilon > 0 some N makes |f_n(x)-f(x)| = N and all x in E.

Encyclopedia

union of sets 并集

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The union of A and B contains exactly the objects belonging to at least one of them.

unique ergodicity 唯一遍历性

Dynamical Systems · Graduate

A continuous self-map of a nonempty compact metric space is uniquely ergodic when it has exactly one invariant Borel probability measure.

Encyclopedia

unique factorization domain 唯一分解环

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A domain in which each nonzero nonunit factors into irreducibles uniquely up to factor order and multiplication by units.

unit of a ring 环的可逆元

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An element $u$ in a unital ring admitting $v$ with $uv=vu=1$.

unit of an adjunction 单位

Category Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For $F\dashv G$, the natural transformation $\eta:1_C\Rightarrow GF$ whose component corresponds to $1_{FX}$ under the adjunction bijection.

unital ring 含幺环

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An abelian additive group with associative multiplication, two-sided distributivity, and a multiplicative identity.

universal covering space 泛复迭空间

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A covering with path connected and simply connected total space.

universal property 泛性质

Category Theory · Graduate

A characterization of specified object-and-map data by a unique factorization for every competing compatible object-and-map datum.

Encyclopedia

universal quantifier 全称量词

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate

$\forall x\,\varphi$ holds under an assignment in a structure precisely when $\varphi$ holds for every domain value assigned to $x$.

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

A finite extension $L/K$ of finite extensions of $\mathbb Q_p$ having ramification index $e(L/K)=1$.
