Lebesgue measure 勒贝格测度

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate

Lebesgue measure on R^n is the complete measure obtained by restricting the countable rectangular-cover outer measure to the sets satisfying the Carathéodory splitting criterion.

Lebesgue outer measure 外测度

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The Lebesgue outer measure of any E in R^n is the infimum of sums of rectangular volumes over all countable rectangle families covering E.

Li–Yorke chaos Li-Yorke混沌

Dynamical Systems · Graduate · Short definition only

A continuous map of a metric space is Li–Yorke chaotic when it admits an uncountable set in which every distinct pair satisfies $\liminf_n d(T^nx,T^ny)=0$ and $\limsup_n d(T^nx,T^ny)>0$.

Distinct points $x,y$ in a metric dynamical system with $\liminf_n d(T^nx,T^ny)=0$ and $\limsup_n d(T^nx,T^ny)>0$.

limit inferior of a real sequence 数列下极限

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The limit inferior of a real sequence a_n is the extended-real limit of the increasing tail infima inf_{k>=n} a_k.

limit of a real sequence 数列极限

A real sequence has limit a if, for every positive epsilon, all terms beyond some index lie within epsilon of a.

The limit superior of a real sequence a_n is the extended-real limit of the decreasing tail suprema sup_{k>=n} a_k.
