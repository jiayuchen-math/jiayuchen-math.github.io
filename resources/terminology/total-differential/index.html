全微分

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For f from an open subset of R^n to R^m, the total differential at a is the linear map A for which f(a+h)-f(a)-Ah=o(|h|), if such A exists.

欧氏空间开集上的映射若在 a 处的增量可写为某个线性映射作用于 h 加上相对于 |h| 的高阶小量，该线性映射称为全微分。

Example

For f(x,y)=xyf(x,y)=xyf(x,y)=xy at (2,3)(2,3)(2,3), df(h,k)=3h+2kdf(h,k)=3h+2kdf(h,k)=3h+2k and the omitted increment is hk=o(h2+k2)hk=o(\sqrt{h^2+k^2})hk=o(h2+k2​).

Usage and distinctions

Partial derivatives test coordinate lines; the remainder condition must hold along every direction simultaneously.
