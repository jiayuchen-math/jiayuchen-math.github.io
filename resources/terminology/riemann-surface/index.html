黎曼曲面

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A connected Hausdorff, second countable topological surface without boundary equipped with an atlas to open subsets of the complex plane whose transition maps are holomorphic; equivalently, a connected complex manifold of complex dimension one.

具有到复平面开集的坐标图册、且坐标变换全纯的连通、Hausdorff、第二可数无边拓扑曲面，即复维数为一的连通复流形。

Example

The sphere C∪{∞}\mathbb C\cup\{\infty\}C∪{∞} has compatible coordinates zzz away from infinity and w=1/zw=1/zw=1/z near infinity.

Usage and distinctions

A Riemann surface has complex coordinate structure; a Riemannian surface has a metric. This entry requires connectedness and excludes boundary points, while some authors use a disconnected convention.
