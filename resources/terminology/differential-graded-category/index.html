dg-范畴

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Research · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: dg category · DG category

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A category enriched in cochain complexes over a commutative ring, with associative unital degree-zero composition that is a map of complexes.

在交换基环上的上链复形中充实、复合满足结合律和单位律且与微分相容的范畴。

Example

A dg algebra is a dg category with one object.

Usage and distinctions

H0H^0H0 discards chain-level data; a triangulated category alone is not a supplied dg enhancement.
