对偶空间

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The continuous dual X* of a normed space X is the vector space of bounded linear scalar-valued functionals on X, equipped with the operator norm.

赋范空间 X 的对偶空间，由 X 上所有有界线性标量泛函组成，并配以算子范数。

Example

For ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2, λ(x)=x3\lambda(x)=x_3λ(x)=x3​ belongs to the continuous dual and has norm 111.

Usage and distinctions

The algebraic dual contains all linear functionals, including discontinuous ones in infinite-dimensional normed spaces.
