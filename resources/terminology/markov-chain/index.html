Example

For P=(0.80.20.40.6)P=\begin{pmatrix}0.8&0.2\\0.4&0.6\end{pmatrix}P=(0.80.4​0.20.6​), starting from state 000 gives probability 0.280.280.28 of state 111 after two steps.

Usage and distinctions

The Markov property is conditional prediction from the present, not independence of successive states. Time homogeneity is a further restriction; this record uses it and finite states explicitly.
