数列上极限

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The limit superior of a real sequence a_n is the extended-real limit of the decreasing tail suprema sup_{k>=n} a_k.

实数列的上极限是其各尾部上确界构成的单调递减列的扩展实数极限，也可等于正无穷或负无穷。

Example

For an=(−1)n+1/na_n=(-1)^n+1/nan​=(−1)n+1/n, lim sup⁡an=1\limsup a_n=1limsupan​=1 and lim inf⁡an=−1\liminf a_n=-1liminfan​=−1.

Usage and distinctions

It records the eventual upper envelope; it is not the supremum of all terms, which includes early outliers.
