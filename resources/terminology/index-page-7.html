Fatou's lemma Fatou引理

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a sequence of nonnegative measurable functions on a measure space, the integral of the pointwise lower limit is at most the lower limit of their integrals.

Fermat's little theorem 费尔马小定理

Number Theory · Undergraduate

If $p$ is prime and $p\nmid a$, then $a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod p$; equivalently $a^p\equiv a$ for every integer $a$.

Encyclopedia

field 域

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A commutative unital ring with $1\ne0$ in which every nonzero element is a unit.

field extension 域扩张

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A field $L$ together with an injective unital field homomorphism $K\to L$, usually identified with a subfield inclusion.

filtration in probability σ代数流

Probability · Graduate · Short definition only

An increasing family $(\mathcal F_t)$ of sub-sigma-algebras of a probability space's event sigma-algebra, indexed by ordered times: $s\le t$ implies $\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t$.

finite field 有限域

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A field with finitely many elements; its cardinality is $p^n$ for a prime $p$ and positive integer $n$.

finite simple graph 简单图

Combinatorics · Undergraduate

A finite simple graph is a pair (V,E) with V finite and E a set of two-element subsets of V; edges are undirected, with no loops or repetitions.

Encyclopedia

finitely generated module 有限生成模

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate · Short definition only

An $R$-module spanned by a finite list of elements under finite $R$-linear combinations.

first-countable space C1 可数公理

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A space in which every point has a countable basis of open neighborhoods: every open neighborhood contains a member of that basis.

first-order compactness theorem 紧致性定理

Foundations & Logic · Graduate

A set of classical first-order sentences has a model if and only if every finite subset has a model.

Encyclopedia

first-order completeness theorem 完全性定理

Foundations & Logic · Graduate

For a sound complete classical first-order calculus, every semantic consequence of a sentence set has a finite derivation from it.

Encyclopedia

first-order formula 合式公式

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A finite expression of a fixed first-order language built from atomic formulas using its permitted connectives and quantifiers.

first-order sentence 语句

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A formula of a first-order language with no free variable occurrences.

first-order structure 结构

Foundations & Logic · Graduate · Short definition only

A nonempty domain equipped with interpretations of a first-order language’s constants, functions and relations of the prescribed arities.

first-order term 项

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An expression naming a domain value, built from variables, constants and correctly applied function symbols in a first-order language.

fixed point in dynamics 不动点

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a map $T:X\to X$, a point $x$ with $T(x)=x$; for a flow, a point fixed by every time map.

flat module 平坦模

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate · Short definition only

A module over a commutative ring for which tensoring preserves every short exact sequence.

flow 流

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate

A jointly continuous action $\phi:\mathbb R\times X\to X$ with $\phi_0=\operatorname{id}$ and $\phi_{t+s}=\phi_t\circ\phi_s$.

formal power series 形式幂级数

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

Over a commutative ring R, a formal power series is a coefficient sequence written ∑ a_n x^n; equality is coefficientwise, addition is termwise, and multiplication uses finite convolution at each degree.

Number Theory · Graduate

For a number field $K$, a nonzero finitely generated $\mathcal O_K$-submodule of $K$; equivalently it becomes an integral ideal after multiplication by a nonzero denominator in $\mathcal O_K$.

An $R$-module with a basis, equivalently a direct sum of copies of $R$ indexed by a set.

For sigma-finite measure spaces X and Y, an integrable real or complex function on their product has integrable sections almost everywhere, and its product integral equals either iterated integral.
