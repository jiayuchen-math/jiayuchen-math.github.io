中心极限定理

Probability · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: CLT

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The classical iid central limit theorem states that for real iid XjX_jXj​ with mean μ\muμ and variance 0<σ2<∞0<\sigma^2<\infty0<σ2<∞, (∑j=1nXj−nμ)/(σn)(\sum_{j=1}^n X_j-n\mu)/(\sigma\sqrt n)(∑j=1n​Xj​−nμ)/(σn​) converges in distribution to N(0,1)N(0,1)N(0,1).

经典独立同分布中心极限定理断言：实随机变量列独立同分布、均值为μ且方差σ²有限并严格为正时，和减去nμ再除以σ√n后依分布收敛于标准正态分布。

Example

For independent Bernoulli trials with fixed 0<p<10<p<10<p<1, (Sn−np)/np(1−p)(S_n-np)/\sqrt{np(1-p)}(Sn​−np)/np(1−p)​ converges in distribution to N(0,1)N(0,1)N(0,1).

Usage and distinctions

The theorem concerns centered, scaled sums, not the original observations becoming normal. This classical iid version does not cover arbitrary dependence or infinite variance.
