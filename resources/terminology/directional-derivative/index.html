方向导数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For f on an open subset of R^n and a specified unit vector v, the directional derivative at a is the finite two-sided limit [f(a+tv)-f(a)]/t as t tends to zero, when it exists.

沿指定单位向量 v，函数的增量商在参数双侧趋于零时若有有限极限，则该极限为此方向的方向导数。

Example

For f(x,y)=x2+y2f(x,y)=x^2+y^2f(x,y)=x2+y2 at (1,2)(1,2)(1,2) and v=(3/5,4/5)v=(3/5,4/5)v=(3/5,4/5), Dvf=2(3/5)+4(4/5)=22/5D_vf=2(3/5)+4(4/5)=22/5Dv​f=2(3/5)+4(4/5)=22/5.

Usage and distinctions

Unit directions measure change per unit distance; allowing arbitrary vectors scales the parameter derivative by their lengths.
