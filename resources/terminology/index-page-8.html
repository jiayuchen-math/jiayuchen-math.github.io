full functor 全函子

Category Theory · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A functor inducing a surjective map on each hom-set for a fixed pair of source objects.

function between sets 映射

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For specified sets A and B, an assignment giving each member of A exactly one member of B.

functor 函子

Category Theory · Undergraduate

For small C and locally small D, the category whose objects are functors C→D and whose morphisms are natural transformations.

The group of loops at a chosen point modulo homotopy fixing the endpoints, with multiplication given here by traversing the first loop then the second.

fundamental theorem of arithmetic 算术基本定理

Number Theory · Undergraduate

Every integer greater than one is a finite product of positive primes, unique up to the order of its factors.

fundamental theorem of calculus 微积分基本定理

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate

For continuous real f on [a,b], the function G(x)=integral_a^x f(t) dt has derivative f(x) in (a,b); if F is any antiderivative of f on that interval, continuous at the endpoints, then integral_a^b f=F(b)-F(a).

fundamental unit of a real quadratic field 实二次域的基本单位

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For a real quadratic field $K$, a unit $\varepsilon$ generating $\mathcal O_K^\times/\{\pm1\}$, so every unit is $\pm\varepsilon^n$ for some $n\in\mathbb Z$.

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a differentiable real scalar function f on an open subset of Euclidean R^n, its gradient at a is the vector of its coordinate partial derivatives, satisfying Df(a)h=gradient f(a) dot h.

graph isomorphism 图的同构

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An isomorphism of simple graphs G=(V,E) and H=(W,F) is a bijection f:V→W such that uv is in E if and only if f(u)f(v) is in F for each distinct u,v.

For a smooth real function H on a symplectic manifold with form omega, the unique smooth vector field X_H satisfying contraction of omega with X_H equals dH; this entry uses the positive-dH sign convention.
