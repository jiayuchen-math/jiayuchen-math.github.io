abelian group 交换群

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A group whose operation satisfies $ab=ba$ for every pair of elements.

absolute convergence of a numerical series 绝对收敛

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A real or complex numerical series sum a_n is absolutely convergent when the nonnegative series sum |a_n| has a finite sum.

adapted stochastic process 适应过程

Probability · Graduate · Short definition only

Relative to a filtration $(\mathcal F_t)$, a process $(X_t)$ is adapted when $X_t$ is $\mathcal F_t$-measurable at every time $t$.

addition principle 加法原理

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For finitely many pairwise disjoint finite sets, the cardinality of their union equals the sum of their cardinalities.

additive combinatorics 加法组合学

Combinatorics · Research

Additive combinatorics studies arithmetic configurations, additive growth, and structural inverse questions for finite sets, especially in integers and abelian groups.

adele ring of a number field 数域的阿代尔环

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For a number field $K$, the ring of tuples $(x_v)\in\prod_v K_v$ such that $x_v\in\mathcal O_v$ at all but finitely many finite places, with the restricted product topology.

adjunction 伴随对

Category Theory · Graduate

Functors F:C→D and G:D→C together with bijections Hom_D(FX,Y)≅Hom_C(X,GY) natural in X and Y.

Encyclopedia

algebraic element over a field 代数元

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An element $\alpha$ of an extension $L/K$ annihilated by a nonzero polynomial in $K[x]$.

algebraic integer 代数整数

Number Theory · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A complex number satisfying a monic polynomial with coefficients in $\mathbb Z$.

almost complex structure 近复结构

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Short definition only

A smooth real bundle endomorphism of the tangent bundle of a smooth manifold whose square is minus the identity on every tangent space.

almost everywhere 几乎处处

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A property holds mu-almost everywhere on X when there is a measurable null set N such that the property holds at every point of X minus N.

almost sure convergence 几乎必然收敛

Probability · Undergraduate

On one probability space, $X_n\to X$ almost surely if $P(\{\omega:\lim_nX_n(\omega)=X(\omega)\})=1$.

alpha-limit set α极限点集

Dynamical Systems · Graduate · Short definition only

For a homeomorphism $T$, $\alpha(x)$ is the set of limits of $T^{-n_k}x$ along sequences $n_k\to\infty$.

annihilator of a module 模的零化子

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate · Short definition only

For a module $M$ over a commutative ring $R$, the ideal $\{r:rM=0\}$.

antiderivative of a real function 原函数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An antiderivative of f on an open real interval I is a differentiable function F on I with F'(x)=f(x) at every point of I.

Banach space Banach空间

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate

A Banach space is a real or complex normed vector space in which every Cauchy sequence for the norm distance converges to an element of the space.

basis for a topology 拓扑基

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A family of open subsets such that every open subset is a union of members of that family.

basis of a vector space 向量空间的基

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A linearly independent family spanning the vector space by finite linear combinations.

Bernoulli distribution Bernoulli分布

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A distribution supported on $\{0,1\}$ with $P(X=1)=p$ and $P(X=0)=1-p$, where $0\le p\le1$.

bijective function 双射

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A function that is both injective and surjective, equivalently one admitting a two-sided inverse.

binomial coefficient 二项式系数

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For integers n ≥ 0 and 0 ≤ k ≤ n, the binomial coefficient counts the k-element subsets of an n-element set and equals n!/(k!(n−k)!). It is defined as zero for integer k outside that range.

binomial distribution 二项分布

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For an integer $n\ge0$ and $0\le p\le1$, the law of the sum of $n$ independent Bernoulli$(p)$ variables; its mass is $\binom nk p^k(1-p)^{n-k}$ for $0\le k\le n$.
