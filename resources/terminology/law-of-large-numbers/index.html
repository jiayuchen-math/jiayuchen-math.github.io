大数定律

Probability · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: LLN

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

In the iid integrable setting, the sample averages n−1∑j=1nXjn^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^n X_jn−1∑j=1n​Xj​ converge to E[X1]E[X_1]E[X1​] almost surely (strong law), and therefore in probability (weak law), assuming real iid XjX_jXj​ and E∣X1∣<∞E|X_1|<\inftyE∣X1​∣<∞.

对独立同分布且绝对一阶矩有限的实随机变量，样本均值几乎必然收敛于共同数学期望（强大数定律），从而也依概率收敛（弱大数定律）。

Example

For iid Bernoulli observations with parameter ppp, the observed success fraction converges almost surely to ppp.

Usage and distinctions

This is the iid finite-mean formulation of a family of laws. Finite variance is not required here; the classical normal central limit theorem additionally assumes finite positive variance.
