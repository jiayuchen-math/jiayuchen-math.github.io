quadratic number field 二次数域

Number Theory · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A degree-two extension of $\mathbb Q$, equivalently $\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$ for a squarefree integer $d\ne0,1$.

quotient group 商群

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a normal subgroup $N$ of $G$, the group of cosets with $(gN)(hN)=ghN$.

quotient map 商映射

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A surjective map q of topological spaces such that a subset of the target is open exactly when its inverse image under q is open.

quotient module 商模

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate · Short definition only

For a submodule $N\le M$, the additive quotient $M/N$ with scalar action $r(m+N)=rm+N$.

quotient ring 商环

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For an ideal $I$ of a commutative ring $R$, the cosets $R/I$ with addition and multiplication induced by representatives.

quotient set 商集

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The set of equivalence classes of a specified equivalence relation on a set X.

quotient topology 商拓扑

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate

For a surjection q from a topological space X onto a set Y, the topology in which U is open in Y exactly when its inverse image under q is open in X.

Encyclopedia

ramification index of a local number-field extension 局部数域扩张的分歧指数

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For a finite extension $L/K$ of finite extensions of $\mathbb Q_p$, the integer $e$ determined by $\mathfrak m_K\mathcal O_L=\mathfrak m_L^e$.

random variable 随机变量

Probability · Undergraduate

A real-valued measurable map $X:(\Omega,\mathcal F)\to(\mathbb R,\mathcal B(\mathbb R))$ on a probability space.

Encyclopedia

rank of a linear map 线性映射的秩

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The dimension of the image of a linear map, measured over its scalar field.

rank–nullity theorem 维数公式

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a linear map $T:V\to W$ with $V$ finite-dimensional, $\dim V=\dim\ker T+\dim\operatorname{im}T$.

real projective space 实射影空间

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a nonnegative integer n, the space of one-dimensional real linear subspaces of real (n+1)-space, topologized as the quotient of nonzero vectors by multiplication by nonzero real scalars.

recurrence relation 递推关系

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A recurrence relation specifies each term of a sequence after a stated starting index in terms of earlier terms; an associated recursive specification also gives enough initial data to determine the sequence.

reduced residue system 简化剩余系

Number Theory · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For $m\ge2$, a set of integers representing each invertible congruence class modulo $m$ exactly once.

regular covering space 正则复迭空间

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A covering map for which the deck-transformation group acts transitively on each fiber, so any two points over the same base point are related by a deck transformation.

regular space T3 分离公理

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A space in which a point and a disjoint closed subset can be enclosed in disjoint open sets; T1 is not included in this record.

removable complex singularity 可去奇点

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An isolated singularity a of a holomorphic f is removable if a value can be assigned at a so that f extends holomorphically to the whole disk.

removable discontinuity 可去间断点

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

At an accumulation point a of a real domain, a finite punctured limit exists but the function is absent or has a different value at a.

representable functor 可表函子

Category Theory · Graduate

A set-valued functor naturally isomorphic to Hom(-,A) in the contravariant case, or Hom(A,-) in the covariant case.

Encyclopedia

residue at an isolated singularity 留数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate

If f is holomorphic on a punctured disk centered at a, its residue at a is the coefficient of (z-a)^(-1) in the Laurent expansion on that disk.

Encyclopedia

residue degree of a local number-field extension 局部数域扩张的惯性指数

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For a finite extension $L/K$ of $p$-adic local fields, $f(L/K)=[\mathcal O_L/\mathfrak m_L:\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak m_K]$.

retraction onto a subspace 收缩

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a subspace A of a topological space X, a continuous map from X to A whose restriction to A is the identity.

Riemann curvature tensor 曲率张量

Geometry & Topology · Graduate

For a smooth Riemannian metric with Levi–Civita connection, the tensor R(X,Y)Z = ∇X∇Y Z − ∇Y∇X Z − ∇[X,Y]Z; pairing its output with the metric gives its covariant four-tensor form.

Encyclopedia

Riemann integral 黎曼积分

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

For a bounded real function on [a,b], the Riemann integral is the common value of the supremum of its lower partition sums and the infimum of its upper partition sums, when those values agree.
