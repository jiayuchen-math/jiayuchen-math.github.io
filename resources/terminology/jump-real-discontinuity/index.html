跳跃间断点

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A real function has a jump at a when both finite one-sided limits exist and are unequal.

实函数在 a 处左右极限都存在且有限、但不相等，则 a 为跳跃间断点。

Example

1[0,∞)\mathbf1_{[0,\infty)}1[0,∞)​ jumps from 000 to 111 at 000.

Usage and distinctions

Changing the value at a cannot make unequal side limits agree.
