Meaning in context

For a probability-preserving TTT and f∈L1(μ)f\in L^1(\mu)f∈L1(μ), the averages n−1∑j=0n−1f(Tjx)n^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}f(T^jx)n−1∑j=0n−1​f(Tjx) converge almost everywhere to E[f∣I]E[f\mid\mathcal I]E[f∣I], with I\mathcal II the invariant sigma-algebra; for ergodic TTT the limit is ∫f dμ\int f\,d\mu∫fdμ.

在概率空间 (X,B,μ)(X,\mathcal B,\mu)(X,B,μ) 上，若可测变换 TTT 保持 μ\muμ 且 f∈L1(μ)f\in L^1(\mu)f∈L1(μ)，则轨道平均几乎处处收敛于 E[f∣I]E[f\mid\mathcal I]E[f∣I]，其中 I\mathcal II 为不变σ代数；若 TTT 遍历，极限为 ∫f dμ\int f\,d\mu∫fdμ。

Example

For a cyclic permutation of rrr equally weighted states, the orbit average equals the arithmetic mean of the rrr observable values.

Usage and distinctions

The nonergodic limit need not be constant; the theorem is pointwise almost everywhere, unlike a bare norm-convergence statement.
