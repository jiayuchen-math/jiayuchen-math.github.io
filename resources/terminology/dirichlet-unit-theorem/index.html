狄利克雷单位定理

Number Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

If a number field has r1r_1r1​ real embeddings and r2r_2r2​ conjugate pairs of complex embeddings, then OK×≅μK×Zr1+r2−1\mathcal O_K^\times\cong\mu_K\times\mathbb Z^{r_1+r_2-1}OK×​≅μK​×Zr1​+r2​−1, where μK\mu_KμK​ is its group of roots of unity.

若数域有 r1r_1r1​ 个实嵌入和 r2r_2r2​ 对共轭复嵌入，则 OK×≅μK×Zr1+r2−1\mathcal O_K^\times\cong\mu_K\times\mathbb Z^{r_1+r_2-1}OK×​≅μK​×Zr1​+r2​−1，其中 μK\mu_KμK​ 为单位根群。

Example

For Q(2)\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)Q(2​) the unit rank is 2+0−1=12+0-1=12+0−1=1.

Usage and distinctions

The rank uses r1+r2r_1+r_2r1​+r2​, whereas the field degree is r1+2r2r_1+2r_2r1​+2r2​; roots of unity are the finite torsion part.
