Lp空间

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a measure space and 1<=p<infinity, Lp consists of measurable real or complex functions with finite integral of |f|^p, identified when equal almost everywhere, with norm (integral |f|^p)^(1/p). L-infinity instead uses the essential-supremum norm.

给定测度空间，1≤p<∞ 时，Lp 空间由 p 次绝对值可积函数的几乎处处相等类组成，范数为该积分的 p 次方根；p=∞ 时用本性上确界。

Usage and distinctions

The equivalence classes remove changes on null sets; literal pointwise functions would give only a seminorm.
