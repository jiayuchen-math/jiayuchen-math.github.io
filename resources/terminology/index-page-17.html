Riemann zeta function 黎曼 ζ 函数

Number Theory · Graduate

The function $\zeta(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-s}$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>1$, continued meromorphically to $\mathbb C$ with a simple pole at $1$.

Encyclopedia

Riemannian geodesic 测地线

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate

A smooth parametrized curve on a Riemannian manifold whose velocity is parallel along the curve with respect to the Levi–Civita connection, so its covariant acceleration is zero; the parameter is affine.

Encyclopedia

Riemannian metric 黎曼度量

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate

A smooth symmetric covariant two-tensor on a smooth real manifold whose value at every point is a positive definite bilinear form on that point's tangent space.

Encyclopedia

right-continuous real function 右连续函数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A real function is right-continuous at a if its limit along domain points decreasing to a equals f(a).

ring of integers of a number field 数域的整数环

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For a number field $K$, $\mathcal O_K$ is the subring of elements integral over $\mathbb Z$.

ring of p-adic integers p 进整数环

Number Theory · Graduate · Short definition only

For prime $p$, $\mathbb Z_p=\{x\in\mathbb Q_p:v_p(x)\ge0\}$, equivalently the inverse limit of $\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z$ under reduction.

Rouché's theorem Rouché定理

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

If f and g are holomorphic near the closure of a bounded domain with piecewise smooth boundary and |g-f|<|f| on that boundary, then f and g have the same number of interior zeros, with multiplicity.

sample space 样本空间

Probability · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The set $\Omega$ of possible outcomes in a specified probability model.

satisfiability 可满足

Foundations & Logic · Graduate · Short definition only

A set of first-order sentences is satisfiable if one structure satisfies all of them together.

scheme 概形

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate

A locally ringed space in which every point has an open neighborhood isomorphic, as a locally ringed space, to the prime spectrum of a commutative unital ring equipped with its structure sheaf.

Encyclopedia

Schwartz function 速降函数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Graduate · Short definition only

A Schwartz function on R^n is a smooth complex-valued function phi such that sup_x |x^alpha partial^beta phi(x)| is finite for every pair of multi-indices alpha,beta.

second derivative 二阶导数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

If f is differentiable near a and f' is differentiable at a, the second derivative f''(a) is the derivative of f' there.

second-countable space C2 可数公理

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A topological space whose topology has a countable basis.

semiflow 半动力系统

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A jointly continuous action $\phi:[0,\infty)\times X\to X$ satisfying $\phi_0=\operatorname{id}$ and $\phi_{t+s}=\phi_t\circ\phi_s$ for $s,t\ge0$.

sensitive dependence on initial conditions 敏感依赖于初始条件

Dynamical Systems · Graduate · Short definition only

For a map of a metric space, some $\delta>0$ has the property that for every $x$ and every neighborhood $U$ of $x$, there exist $y\in U$ and $n\ge0$ with $d(T^nx,T^ny)>\delta$.

separable algebraic extension 可分扩张

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Short definition only

An algebraic extension $L/K$ in which every element's minimal polynomial has distinct roots in an algebraic closure.

separable space 可分

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A topological space admitting a countable dense subset.

sequential compactness 列紧

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The property that every sequence in a space has a subsequence converging to a point of that space.

set 集合

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate

A primitive collection in ZF set theory, whose identity is determined by its members.

Encyclopedia

set difference 差

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Short definition only

The difference A∖B consists of members of A that do not belong to B.

sheaf 层

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate

A presheaf of sets on a topological space such that every compatible family of sections on an open cover, including the empty cover, is the restriction of a unique section on the union.

Encyclopedia

short exact sequence of modules 模的短正合列

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate

A sequence $0\to A\xrightarrow{i}B\xrightarrow{p}C\to0$ with $i$ injective, $p$ surjective and $\operatorname{im}i=\ker p$.

Encyclopedia

sigma-finite measure σ有限测度

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A measure mu on X is sigma-finite if X is the union of countably many measurable sets each having finite measure.

simple measurable function 简单函数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A simple function on a measurable space is a real measurable function taking finitely many values, equivalently a finite linear combination of indicators of measurable sets.

simply connected space 单连通

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Short definition only

A nonempty path connected space whose fundamental group at one, hence every, basepoint is trivial.
