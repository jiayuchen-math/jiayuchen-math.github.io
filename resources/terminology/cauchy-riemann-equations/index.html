柯西-黎曼方程

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For f=u+iv on a plane domain, the Cauchy–Riemann equations are u_x=v_y and u_y=-v_x. Under C^1 regularity they characterize holomorphicity.

对平面区域上的 f=u+iv，柯西-黎曼方程为 u_x=v_y、u_y=-v_x；在一阶偏导连续的条件下，它们等价于全纯性。

For z2z^2z2, u=x2−y2u=x^2-y^2u=x2−y2 and v=2xyv=2xyv=2xy, so ux=vy=2xu_x=v_y=2xux​=vy​=2x and uy=−vx=−2yu_y=-v_x=-2yuy​=−vx​=−2y.

Usage and distinctions

Equations satisfied at an isolated point without sufficient differentiability do not establish holomorphicity on a neighborhood.
