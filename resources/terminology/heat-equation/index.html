Meaning in context

The constant-coefficient heat equation is partial_t u=kappa Delta_x u with kappa>0, for a space-time function u that is once time differentiable and twice space differentiable in the classical formulation.

常系数热方程为 u_t=κΔu，其中 κ>0；经典表述要求关于时间一阶、关于空间二阶可微。

Example

u(x,t)=e−4κtcos⁡(2x)u(x,t)=e^{-4\kappa t}\cos(2x)u(x,t)=e−4κtcos(2x) solves it because ut=−4κuu_t=-4\kappa uut​=−4κu and uxx=−4uu_{xx}=-4uuxx​=−4u.

Usage and distinctions

The first-order time derivative produces diffusive decay, unlike the second-order time dynamics of the wave equation.
