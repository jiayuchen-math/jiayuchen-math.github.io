singular cohomology 奇异上同调群

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Short definition only

For an abelian coefficient group A, the cohomology of Hom(C_*(X;Z),A), with coboundary given by precomposing a cochain with the singular boundary.

singular homology 奇异同调

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate

The homology of the free chain complex generated by continuous standard simplices in X, using alternating face boundaries; coefficients are integers unless stated otherwise.

A continuous map from the standard n-simplex with ordered vertices into a topological space, for some nonnegative integer n.

stopping time 停时

Probability · Graduate

For a filtration $(\mathcal F_t)$, a random time $\tau$ valued in $[0,\infty]$ is a stopping time when $\{\tau\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t$ for every $t$.

strong induction 强数学归纳法
