p 进数域

Number Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: field of p-adic numbers

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For prime ppp, Qp\mathbb Q_pQp​ is the completion of Q\mathbb QQ for the absolute value ∣x∣p=p−vp(x)|x|_p=p^{-v_p(x)}∣x∣p​=p−vp​(x).

对素数 ppp，Qp\mathbb Q_pQp​ 是有理数域关于绝对值 ∣x∣p=p−vp(x)|x|_p=p^{-v_p(x)}∣x∣p​=p−vp​(x) 的完备化。

Example

In Q3\mathbb Q_3Q3​, 1+3+32+⋯=−1/21+3+3^2+\cdots=-1/21+3+32+⋯=−1/2.

Usage and distinctions

Despite its name, Qp\mathbb Q_pQp​ is not a number field in the finite-extension-of-Q\mathbb QQ sense.
