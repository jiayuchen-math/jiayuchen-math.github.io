1 Proof, logic, and limit language

1.1 Basic terminology

  • An axiom is accepted as part of the framework in which we work.

1.3 Direct proof, contradiction, and contraposition

Direct proof

To prove a statement of the form

assume that PPP is true and use definitions, axioms, and previously proved results until QQQ follows. This is the default method for most ε\varepsilonε-δ\deltaδ arguments.

Proof by contradiction

To prove a statement SSS:

  1. Assume its negation, ¬S\neg S¬S.

  2. Derive a contradiction, such as R∧¬RR\land\neg RR∧¬R, an impossible inequality, or a conflict with a known theorem.

1.4 Mathematical induction

The Intermediate Value Theorem

Any sequence bounded above has a supremum, and any sequence bounded below has an infimum.
