Lecture notes emphasizing simplicial homology and its technical foundations, followed by singular and cellular methods and the universal coefficient theorem. The recorded version does not develop the cohomology ring.重点讲单纯同调及其技术细节，随后讨论奇异同调、胞腔方法和万有系数定理。这个记录版本没有继续展开上同调环。
